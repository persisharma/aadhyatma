The two ways location is set

📍 GPS → requestDeviceLocation(): permission prompt, then the fix is snapped to the nearest of ~50 bundled cities (source: 'gps'). Denied/timeout keeps the current city and shows a hint.

Tap a city → selectCity(id) (source: 'city').

Either way: state updates, persists to AsyncStorage, and kicks off the festival warm-up.

components/LocationPickerModal.tsx contexts/PanchangLocationContext.tsx → requestDeviceLocation / selectCity panchang/locations.ts → CITIES, nearestCity()

Sunrise 5:40 → 5:23 AM, sunset 7:13 → 7:20 PM — Delhi is east of Ujjain. Tithi/nakshatra are the same here (the boundary didn't fall between the two sunrises). Recomputed on the spot, off the render path.

Festivals: shown approximate, then upgraded

The spinner shows while the Ujjain dates stand in (observancesApproximate). A chunked background scan computes Delhi's dates, persists them per-city, and the list silently upgrades — next launch it's an instant cache hit.

panchang/festivalEngine.ts → resolveObservancesForYearLiveChunked panchang/observanceCache.ts → warmObservanceCache (AsyncStorage) panchang/observanceStore.ts → subscribe/notify
