How it behaves
• Tap a chapter you’ve partly read → it opens right where you left off (here, verse 12).
• The ⇤ button sits left of the bookmark ♡ and share ⤴ icons, matching their round parchment style.
• It’s hidden at verse 1 (nothing to jump back to) and fades in for any later verse.
• One tap animates the pager back to the first verse — no repeated left-swiping.
