# Dhyāna — Guided Session (English) · ~8 minutes

**Status:** final draft — requires teacher/doctrinal review before recording (PRD-15 §8).
**Voice direction:** slow, warm, unhurried; the silence after each segment matters as much as the words. Total duration ≈ 8:05.

Structure follows Gītā chapter 6 (Dhyāna Yoga): sthiti → prāṇa awareness → OM anchor → stillness → samāpti.

| # | Time | Segment | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | 0:00 | **Opening bell** | *(one soft bell strike, ~8 s)* |
| 1 | 0:12 | Sthiti (posture) | Sit comfortably. Let your spine be gently upright, shoulders soft, hands resting in your lap. Slowly close your eyes. |
| 2 | 0:50 | Prāṇa awareness | Now bring your attention to the breath. There is nothing to change — simply watch the breath as it comes and goes. |
| 3 | 1:40 | Settling | With each breath, allow the body and the mind to settle a little more. |
| 4 | 2:30 | OM anchor | Now bring OM to mind. Breathing in — stillness; breathing out — silently, within… OM. |
| 5 | 3:10 | OM practice | With every out-breath, keep repeating OM silently. If thoughts arise, it does not matter — gently return to OM. |
| 6 | 4:20 | Into stillness | Now sit for a while in silence. As the Gītā says — like the flame of a lamp in a windless place, the still mind does not flicker. |
| — | 4:45–7:05 | **Stillness (silence)** | *(about two and a quarter minutes of silence)* |
| 7 | 5:55 | Mid-cue (very soft) | Simply… still, with OM. |
| 8 | 7:10 | Samāpti (closing) | Now slowly bring your awareness back to the body. Rub your palms together, place them gently over your eyes, and slowly open your eyes. |
| 9 | 7:40 | Śānti | Om śānti, śānti, śānti. |
| — | 7:52 | **Closing bell** | *(three soft strikes, fading)* |

**Notes**

- Segment 6 references Gītā 6.19 — "yathā dīpo nivāta-stho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā…"
- Segment 7 is near-whisper level — it must not break the stillness.
