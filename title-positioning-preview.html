भाषा शीर्षक स्थिति · Language Title Positioning

How catalog & category titles re-order and re-emphasise to follow the reader's selected language. Hindi mode is unchanged; the change is visible when the language is switched to English.

What changed: previously every listing showed Hindi on top in the larger, bolder font regardless of the language toggle — and even after the first pass, Hindi stayed semibold while English was a thin italic, so Hindi still dominated. Now weight & emphasis follow the slot, not the language: the primary line (active language) is always semibold/upright at the larger size, and the other language drops to a lighter supporting line beneath. Same data, both languages shown; order, weight & size all follow lang.
