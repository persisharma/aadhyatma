What you're seeing

  • Home / Index — single scroll of sacred texts. Hanuman Chalisa is live; others show a muted "Soon" badge so the library looks alive from day one.
  • Reader pages — one doha or chaupai per screen with its Hindi meaning just below. Each page picks a random background from the 3 vintage sketches you provided, with a parchment overlay so text stays fully readable.
  • Navigation — swipe left/right to go between the 43 verses (2 opening dohas → 40 chaupais → closing doha). Pager dots at the bottom show broad progress.
  • Typography — Noto Serif Devanagari for Hindi (warm, book-like) paired with Cormorant Garamond for the Latin secondary labels.
