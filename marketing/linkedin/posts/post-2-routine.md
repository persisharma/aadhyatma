When I first built this app, it was just for me — a few grounding minutes with the Gita on my commute.

But "a few minutes daily" quietly raises a harder question: which few minutes?

Some mornings I'd open it and just… scroll. Too many choices, no rhythm. And I realised most of us already have a rhythm we half-follow — Monday for Shiva, Tuesday for Hanuman, Saturday for Shani — we just don't always have it in front of us.

So the app now lets you build your own daily routine — your sadhana:

• Pick what you want — a Gita chapter, a chalisa, an aarti, a stotram, or a japam with a target count
• Choose your mode — the same practice every day, or a different one for each weekday
• Weekday mode suggests the deity for the day — Mon → Shiva, Tue → Hanuman … Sun → Surya
• Each morning, a simple "Today's Practice" list shows exactly what to do — tick it off as you go

No streak-shaming, no noise. Just a calm, repeatable order to the day, assembled from the texts already in the app.

Honestly, this is the feature that changed my own habit the most. I stopped deciding what to read every morning, and just… read.

It's called Vedansh, built in React Native.
👉 https://persisharma.github.io/get-vedansh/ (iPhone & Android)

If you've been wanting a small, steady spiritual habit — not a big resolution, just a few honest minutes a day — I'd love for you to try it 🙏
