A few months ago I shared a small app I'd built to read the Gita on my commute, instead of scrolling reels.

Since then, something I didn't plan for happened. I started noticing how my wife — and so many people I know who keep vrats and fasts — actually carry their devotion through the week.

And it was always the same small frictions:

• "Which day is the vrat this month?" — checked, forgotten, checked again.
• Mid-pooja, phone in hand, hunting for the right katha to read.
• Realising the fast was today — a little too late.

None of it is about lack of faith. It's just that the date, the story, and the reminder all live in different places. So I tried to bring them into one quiet corner of the phone.

The app now has:
• Panchang — tithi, nakshatra, sunrise & sunset, Brahma Muhurta, for your city
• A vrat & festival calendar you can browse and search — Karwa Chauth, Ekadashi, Satyanarayan and more
• Vrat kathas in Hindi + English, ready to read during pooja
• Follow the vrats you keep — they gather in one "My Vrat" list with the next date and a countdown
• Gentle reminders — the evening before, and again on the morning of — so nothing slips by
• Works fully offline. No account, no clutter.

What stayed with me was watching my wife set a reminder the night before a vrat, then not think about it again until the phone quietly nudged her in the morning. That was the whole point.

It's called Vedansh, built in React Native.
👉 https://persisharma.github.io/get-vedansh/ (iPhone & Android)

Still not trying to build the next big app — just hoping it takes a little of the mental load off the practices that matter to people 🙏
