```
Pitru Paksha begins — what did Rama offer his father?

Not gold, not grain: a cupped handful of water, facing south.

In the Ayodhya Kanda, Bharata reaches Chitrakoot with news of Dasharatha's death. Rama goes down to the Mandakini, fills his hands with water — "Father, may this reach you." Nothing royal in the forest; a pinda of ingudi pulp — "what we eat, that we offer you."

That bank is still there — Ramghat, Chitrakoot, on the Mandakini.
```
