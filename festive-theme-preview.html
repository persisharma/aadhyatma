पर्व के दिन घर कैसे सजे? — four treatments

On each of the 18 catalog festivals (the same festiveReminders.ts list that drives the morning notification), Home gets a festive layer. Everything below is drawn in the app's own manuscript grammar — no emoji (the §42 rule), warm palette only (§2), and motion held to the §11 restraint: soft falls and fades, never scale-pops or confetti.

Option A · Ambient

Toran — a garland over the doorway

तोरण — दिन भर, बिना रोक-टोक
No interruption All day Effort: S–M Motion-rule safe

A marigold-and-leaf garland strung below the wordmark — the way a doorway is dressed for a festival — with a quiet chip naming the day. It sways ±0.7° on a slow loop; under reduce-motion it simply hangs still.

  • Feels like: the house is decorated; nothing demands a tap.
  • Code: one FestiveToran View-composition (same grammar as the deity glyphs), mounted in HomeScreen when today resolves to a catalog festival.
  • Risk: lowest. Doesn't move any existing layout below it if given reserved height (the TodayStrip lesson).
Option B · Content-forward

Utsav banner — the greeting as a card

उत्सव पट्टिका — शुभकामना + पाठ का न्यौता
No interruption All day Effort: S Overlaps FOR TODAY

The notification's own copy, resident on Home for the day: a gradient card above the Today strip with a flickering diya, the greeting, and a filled पढ़ें button to the reading. RoutineBanner grammar, so it costs almost nothing to build.

  • Feels like: the app greets you the moment you arrive.
  • Honest concern: the FOR TODAY row's first card already says आज दीपावली है · पढ़ें — two cards, same festival, same CTA, ~90 px apart. If we take B, the FOR TODAY festival card should demote or dedupe.
  • Risk: pushes everything below it down all day (Home real estate is contested — §18).
Option C · Moment

Pushpa-varsha — flowers on first open

पुष्प-वर्षा — पर्व के दिन पहली बार खोलने पर, एक बार
Once per festival day ~6 s, tap-through Effort: XS Already sanctioned

The existing routine-completion flower shower, re-aimed: first Home open on a festival day plays the marigold fall with the festival's greeting as the caption, plus the success haptic. Once per day, persisted; pointer-events pass through, so it never blocks a tap.

  • Feels like: the devas noticed the day. The one animated moment §11 already blesses.
  • Code: reuse RoutineCelebration with a caption prop + a @vedansh/festive-celebrated date key. Days of work: well under one.
  • Watch-out: if the user's routine also completes today, two showers could stack — needs the same no-stacking gate the §6.2 surfaces use.
Option D · Full skin

Deep utsav — diyas, toran, gilded chrome

दीप-सज्जा — पूरा घर सोने में
All day Effort: L Largest QA surface

The maximal read of "fancy": header bathed in a deeper gold, corner diyas with live flames, the toran, and a gilded tab bar — Home visibly transformed for the day.

  • Feels like: a true festival takeover. Unmissable.
  • Honest concern: it re-tints surfaces that carry text, so every affected contrast pair re-enters colors.contrast.test.ts; and a day-long glow dilutes what the pushpa-varsha keeps special. It also leans against the app's "reverent, not confetti" stance.
  • If chosen: scope to the header band only (no tab-bar tint) and hold flames still under reduce-motion.

Recommendation: C + A

Pushpa-varsha on first open, toran for the rest of the day. The shower gives the celebration its moment — using the one animation the design language already blesses, with the festival's own greeting as the caption — and the garland keeps the house feeling dressed after the flowers settle, without spending any Home real estate or interrupting anything. B's job is already done by the FOR TODAY festival card; D can stay in the drawer unless we want a Diwali-tier "major festival" flag later (the catalog's fame order could gate it to the top 5).

To decide before build:

  1. All 18 festivals, or majors only? A toran on Vivah Panchami may feel like crying wolf; we could gate the theme to marker: 'star' festivals (~9) and let the rest keep just the FOR TODAY card.
  2. Shower + routine-completion collision: same day, two showers — play festive first and suppress the second, or space them?
  3. Does the toran persist on scroll (pinned header) or scroll away with the wordmark? Prototype shows it scrolling away — calmer.
