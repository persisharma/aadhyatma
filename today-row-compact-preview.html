The नित्य साधना tile taken literally. It saves the least of the three (the caption sits outside the 72 pt tile), and at 110 pt wide the tile cannot hold a text name — हनुमान चालीसा already truncates, and विष्णु सहस्रनाम अंश would be unreadable. It also reads as a second CATEGORIES grid 200 pt above the real one.

Why B

The नित्य साधना tile is the right instinct — low, calm, no chrome — but its literal form (D) is a launcher for a fixed word like जप, not a carrier for text titles that run 6–20 Devanagari clusters. B keeps what makes that tile feel light (one row, icon + name, no button) while staying a card that can name a text.

  • The पढ़ें pill goes in every option. It was never a button — the whole card is the tap target — so it cost 30 pt for a chevron's worth of meaning.
  • The blurb stays in B because it is not decoration: on a festival day it becomes आज दीपावली है, which is the line that ties the morning notification, the toran, and this row to the same festival (festiveReminders.test.ts fails if they disagree).
  • Width 292 → 248 so a viewport shows more of the day's recommendations, not just a shorter row.

Geometry is 1 dp = 1 px against mobile/src/theme/colors.ts and spacing.ts. Web font metrics differ slightly from the bundled Noto Serif Devanagari, so card heights are accurate to roughly ±2 pt.
