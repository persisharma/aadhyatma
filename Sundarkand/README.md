# Sundarkand

Markdown edition of the **Sundarkand** (पञ्चम सोपान) of Goswami Tulsidas's *Shri Ramcharitmanas*.

## Files

- `sundarkand-hi-en.md` — full text (Devanagari) with Hindi भावार्थ and English meaning per verse.

## Counts

- Shlokas (mangalacharan): 3
- Chaupai groups: 271
- Dohas (incl. क/ख sub-numbered): 62
- Sortha: 1
- Chhand: 6
- Total verses: 343

## Sources

- Devanagari text: Tulsidas's public-domain composition (~1574 CE), cross-referenced against [sanskritdocuments.org](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_otherlang_hindi/sundarakaaNDa.html).
- Hindi भावार्थ and English translations: paraphrases adapted from [ramcharitmanas.info](https://ramcharitmanas.info/collection/sunder-kaand/). Please credit them if you redistribute.

## Stanza structure

Sundarkand has 60 stanzas. Each stanza is a sequence of chaupais ending in a doha — except stanza 12 ends in a *sortha*, and stanzas 39, 49, 56 each end in two sub-numbered dohas (`क` and `ख`). Three Sanskrit shlokas form the opening *mangalacharan*.
