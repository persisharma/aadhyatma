What changed in “After” (English-primary):
• Primary English: Cormorant 700 Bold @ 17px + 0.3px tracking (was 600 SemiBold @ 15px, no tracking) — gives the heading real presence, since Cormorant runs optically light next to Devanagari.
• Secondary Hindi: Noto Serif Devanagari 500 @ 12px, color ink-muted (was 13px, ink-soft) — recedes into a caption instead of competing as a peer.
• The Hindi-primary reference is untouched except its secondary line also moves to 12px / ink-muted for symmetry.
Principle: the two scripts have different optical weight at the same point size, so the primary slot gets a script-aware boost (bigger + bolder for Latin) and the secondary slot is demoted harder (smaller + more muted) — in both language directions.
