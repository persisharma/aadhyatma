Why this shape

  • Opt-in, off by default. Presence of the reminder field on the routine record is the switch — no parallel boolean to drift. Additive optional field ⇒ migration-free.
  • One time per routine (not the shared Daily-Bhakti time the sankalp family reuses): "प्रातः" and "संध्या" routines only make sense at two different times.
  • Weekday-aware caption = the union of item weekdays; empty union warns and schedules nothing. Reuses the shipped TimeStepper control.

Permission is the toggle's job — never the scheduler's

  • Flipping the switch ON while status is undetermined calls the shared requestPermission(); the headless scheduler only schedules when already granted (the shipped Sadhana contract).
  • Honesty rule: reminder persists only after a grant. Tap "Don't Allow" above — the routine saves with the toggle honestly off.
  • permissionState.ts handles the Android quirk: a never-asked POST_NOTIFICATIONS reports denied + canAskAgain; it maps back to undetermined unless the app truly prompted before. Hard denial ⇒ the row becomes a "enable in Settings" banner.

Why RoutineToday, not a reader

  • Payload {type:'routine-reminder', routineId, dateKey}; routing gates on type only, so a stale notice for a deleted routine still lands safely.
  • Identical landing to the shipped sadhana-reminder tap: a lock-screen tap must never run a reader's setProgress effect and clobber the resume position — the user chooses what to open from here.
  • Budget: ROUTINE_REMINDER_CAP = 12 over a 7-day horizon — a deliberately small slice of iOS's shared 64-pending cap, which the seven existing families already over-subscribe in the worst case. Re-arm on every foreground keeps the short window honest.
