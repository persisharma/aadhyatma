Muhurat results — lagna chip & आपके लिए strip fix

The अन्नप्राशन results card, before and after — plus the real question: how much of the personal strip belongs on a list card at all? Data mirrors the Bengaluru screenshot of 19 Aug 2026.

Root cause of the visual bugs: both chips render as styled <Text>. The lagna chip is nested inside the window line's Text run, and iOS ignores borderRadius/overflow/padding on nested Text — it paints a square highlight glued to "AM". The आपके लिए pills have horizontal padding only, and the row wraps its second pill flush under the tiny label. The altitude question: the strip can never re-rank days (annotates, never re-grades — §8.3), so the list is the only place personal fit can steer which day gets picked — remove it entirely and comparing days means opening every detail. But the factor names (वध तारा, चंद्राष्टम) are evidence, and evidence belongs on the day detail (answer → action → evidence). Decision (Aug 2026): (c) SHIPPED — both pills stay on the card; the आपके लिए label takes its own line so the two pills render together on one line beneath it. The compact chip (b) stays on this page as the considered-and-not-chosen alternative. Unchanged in every option: tier, ordering, copy, privacy.

(b) Compact summary chip — considered, NOT chosen. The card keeps exactly one personal signal: a worst-of-two chip — any factor unfavourable → प्रतिकूल, all favourable → अनुकूल, contested-only → मत भिन्न. The factor names move to the day detail (d), where the explainer already lives. This kills the two-red-pills-under-श्रेष्ठ contradiction, keeps cards one line shorter, and preserves the strip's whole point at list level: Sep 14 pops as the one day that also suits you personally — visible here without opening four details. Pure presentation change in MuhuratBalaStrip's row variant; engine, ordering and the §8.3 invariant untouched.
