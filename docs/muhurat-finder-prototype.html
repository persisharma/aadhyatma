4 · Day detail. Rahu Kaal is struck through in place, not hidden — the user sees it was considered. Reminder reuses the shipped local-notification scheduler; share reuses the muhurat share card.

Why this is cheap to build

Everything the finder needs already ships and is already verified. PanchangData gives tithi (with paksha and kshaya), nakshatra, yoga, karana, vara, lunar month and isAdhik; MuhuratDay gives the 8+8 choghadiya, Rahu/Gulika/Yamaganda and Abhijit. The finder adds no astronomy — one new pure module, panchang/eventMuhurat.ts, that reads those two structures and a bundled rule table. Rahu Kaal and the day choghadiya are both exact eighths of the daytime, so excluding a kaal is dropping a slot, not clipping an interval.

What the real run exposed

  • A naive filter is confidently wrong. Without the Chaturmas rule the draft returned 5 "recommended" Griha Pravesh dates between Aug and Oct 2026 — every one of which a purohit would reject. With it: zero, correctly.
  • The season boundary is itself a kshaya tithi. Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026 touches no sunrise; only the shipped kshaya fallback resolves it to 20 Nov. A finder built on a naive tithi lookup would miss the date that unlocks the entire feature.
  • Yield is low, and that's honest. Over 240 days: Griha Pravesh 10 recommended, Vidyarambh 19, Namkaran 42. Scarcity is the product — it is why people search at all.

External validation (7 Aug 2026)

The "zero Griha Pravesh dates in the next 90 days" result was checked against published muhurat lists, since it is a surprising claim. It holds. No source lists any Griha Pravesh date in Aug, Sep or Oct 2026; Hindi sources state the bar directly — चातुर्मास … में गृह प्रवेश करना निषेध होता है — and give the preferred months as Magha, Phalguna, Vaishakha, Jyeshtha. Chaturmas start (25 Jul 2026) matches the engine exactly.

Day-by-day against a published Nov-2026 list (engine run for Ujjain; list is New Delhi):

  • Exact match on 11, 25 and 26 Nov — tithi, nakshatra and weekday all agree.
  • 14 and 20 Nov differ by one nakshatra, and both are days where the nakshatra changes within hours of sunrise (14 Nov: Purvashadha ends 8:22 pm; 20 Nov: Purva Bhadrapada ends 6:54 am). The engine reports the sunrise anga by its documented udaya-vyapini convention; muhurat lists report the anga prevailing during the muhurat window. This is a design finding, not an engine bug — the finder must evaluate angas at the candidate window, not at sunrise, or it will disagree with every published list on early-changeover days.
  • 21 Nov diverges (engine: Dwadashi; list: Trayodashi), but that list is internally inconsistent — it runs Ekadashi → Trayodashi, skipping Dwadashi. The engine is backed by in-repo DrikPanchang fixtures and a 150-chart Swiss Ephemeris corpus; the aggregator is not.

Where the draft was too strict: it lifts the bar at Dev Uthani Ekadashi, so its first dates are 25–26 Nov. Published lists resume at Kartik Shukla post-Diwali and carry 11 and 14 Nov. Under a third reading (month-based: only Ashadha–Ashwin barred, Kartik free) even 30 Oct 2026 would qualify — inside the 90-day window. Three attested conventions, three different answers; only the strict and published readings give zero for this window, and they agree.

Open gates before this ships

  • RULEBOOK §10 content review of the rule tables. The nakshatra/tithi/vara lists here are drafted, not sourced. They are religious content and need ≥2 authoritative concordant sources each, with recension notes — the same bar every text in the app clears.
  • Bhadra is approximated. Detection uses the sunrise karana; Bhadra actually spans half a tithi and can start mid-day. A real Bhadra window solver is a follow-up, or v1 states the limitation plainly.
  • Regional variance. North Indian and South Indian muhurta practice diverge, as do sampradayas. v1 should name the convention it follows (DrikPanchang, as the rest of the Panchang subsystem already does).
  • Vivah is deliberately excluded. Marriage muhurta needs both charts and guna milan, and is the highest-stakes decision a user could take from an app. Out of scope — the disclaimer says so on every screen.
  • Abhijit on Wednesday. Several traditions void it; the engine currently always emits it. Flagged, not decided.
