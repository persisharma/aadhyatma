Home grid math — the one real layout question

The grid is 15 tiles = 5 full rows of 3, deliberately closed by नित्य साधना (design.md §18: appended last "so the grid closes on a full row of 3 rather than an orphan pair"). Adding मुहूर्त makes 16 → one orphan. Options:

  • Shown above: नित्य साधना renders as a full-width closing row — it is the "daily practice" door, and a wide closer reads as intentional. One-line change; no tile removed or reordered.
  • Alternative: accept a single orphan tile until सहस्रनाम (the next planned PRD-A form, design.md §736) lands as tile 17… which still doesn't square; 18 does. Slower and uglier in the interim.
  • Rejected: demoting or removing any existing tile — the user constraint, and the ranked order (§735) is a product decision this feature has no business editing.

मुहूर्त slots after कुंडली because the ranked sequence groups the two Panchang-tab doors (व्रत · कुंडली) right after जप — a muhurat door belongs in that cluster, not with the content categories.
