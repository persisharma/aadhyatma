External validation (7 Aug 2026)

The "zero Griha Pravesh dates in the next 90 days" result was checked against published muhurat lists. It holds. No source lists any Griha Pravesh date in Aug, Sep or Oct 2026; Hindi sources state the bar directly — चातुर्मास … में गृह प्रवेश करना निषेध होता है. Chaturmas start (25 Jul 2026) matches the engine exactly. Day-by-day against a published Nov-2026 list: exact match on 11, 25 and 26 Nov; 14 and 20 Nov differ by one nakshatra, and both are days where the nakshatra changes within hours of sunrise — the engine reports the sunrise anga (udaya-vyapini), muhurat lists report the anga prevailing during the window. A design finding, not an engine bug: the finder must evaluate angas at the candidate window.

DrikPanchang also states its own method — Nakshatra, Weekday, Tithi and Lunar-Month Shuddhi, plus a Muhurta Chintamani / Dharmasindhu rule barring Griha Pravesh while Shukra or Guru Tara are asta. Computed from shipped code: गुरु अस्त 15 Jul – 13 Aug 2026 (11° orb) and शुक्र अस्त 18 – 30 Oct 2026 (10° orb, Venus retrograde, inferior conjunction 24 Oct). 30 Oct sits at 9.71° — barred at a flat 10° orb, clear at the 8° orb some texts use for retrograde Shukra.

Open gates before this ships

  • RULEBOOK §10 content review of the rule tables — ≥2 authoritative concordant sources each, with recension notes.
  • Shukra/Guru asta orb must be stated (10° flat vs 8° retrograde Venus, 11° Jupiter). Strictly, Tara asta is heliacal set/rise, not a fixed orb.
  • Bhadra is approximated — sunrise karana only; needs a window solver or a stated limitation.
  • Chaturmas convention must be picked and named; three readings are attested.
  • Regional variance — name the convention followed (DrikPanchang, as the rest of the subsystem does).
  • Vivah deliberately excluded — needs both charts and guna milan.
  • Abhijit on Wednesday — several traditions void it; the engine always emits it.
