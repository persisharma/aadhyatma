Explore Sheet आज खोजें

On launch, a bottom sheet rises over Home and previews one section this user has never opened. The preview shows real content from that section. One tap opens the section. The sheet stops appearing once every section has been visited.

Read this first

Certain

A launch pop-up goes against a decision the team already made. The rating prompt lost its cold-start trigger in Sept 2026 because it interrupted whatever the user opened the app to do. This sheet needs the guards that prompt lacked: one auto-opening surface per launch, never on a notification or widget launch, and an off switch in Settings.

Decided

Cadence: on launch, at most 2 a day, at least 6 hours apart. A sheet counts toward both limits once it is shown, whether the user opens it or taps "Not now". A day is the phone's local calendar day. The 6-hour gap already stops a sheet at 23:00 from being followed by another just after midnight.

Certain

The app has no record of which sections a user has visited. UserActivityContext logs verse reads and japa. NewContentContext logs individual texts. The visit ledger is new infrastructure.

Existing users start with an empty ledger, so ship it silently one OTA earlier and fill it from data they already have.

Certain

Preview data must not load at launch. The temple registry and other preview sources are deliberately kept out of Home's launch path, and a test enforces the launch budget. The sheet's code and data load only after the sheet decides to open.

Certain

You won't be able to tell whether it works. Meta app events only cover standard activation and carry no feature data, so taps on the sheet never leave the device.

Prototype

Launch the app and the sheet rises after Home settles. Relaunch after 1 hour or 6 hours to see the limits. Tap the button or the preview to open the section. Tap "Not now", ✕ or the dimmed area to dismiss. The preview names come from the bundled data. The hero is a stand-in for each section's existing R2 artwork.

Launch log

    How a launch decides

    1. Stand down when any of these is true: Settings has it off; this is a fresh install's first session (the tour runs instead); another auto-opening surface wants this launch (What's New, update ready, reminder opt-in); the app was opened from a notification, widget or deep link; a sheet was shown less than 6 hours ago; 2 sheets have already been shown today; or the sheet is asleep.
    2. Pool = the 16 sections minus visited, minus any dismissed with "Not now" in the last 14 days, minus sections this device can't use (widgets on an unsupported OS, Vastu without a magnetometer).
    3. No repeat of the last section shown when anything else qualifies.
    4. Score = base priority + today's boost (a festival day lifts Guided Pujas; Pitru Paksha lifts Pitru Smaran) − 10 for each earlier "Not now".
    5. Highest score wins. Ties break on a shuffle seeded at launch.
    6. Wait for Home to settle (after interactions plus about 800ms), then rise. If the user has already left Home by then, skip this launch.
    7. Two "Not now" in a row put the sheet to sleep for 7 days. If the pool is empty, the sheet stops appearing.

    Definitions

    Section
    One of 16 entries in data/discovery/sections.ts. Bottom tabs are left out because the tab bar always shows them. Content categories are left out because NEW badges already cover them.
    Visit
    Landing on any route that belongs to the section, by any route in: the sheet, DISCOVER, a tile, search, a deep link or a notification. Recorded in App.tsx handleRouteChange.
    Launch
    A cold start, or a return to the app after 30+ minutes in the background.
    Limits
    At most 2 sheets per local calendar day, and at least 6 hours between them, measured from when each one was shown. If the phone's clock moves backwards, the 6 hours start again from the current time instead of blocking indefinitely.
    Fallback
    If the ledger fails to load, show no sheet. Never treat every section as unvisited.

    Sheet spec

    Shell
    Same sheet as OnboardingSetupSheet: transparent Modal sliding up over modalBackdrop, a grabber, parchmentHighlight fill, 22 top corners, elevation.overlay, max height 88% with the body in a ScrollView so large text still reaches the button.
    Hero
    132dp band showing the section's existing R2 artwork through CachedArtwork, with the parchment plate as the offline fallback. Progress chip: 3 / 16 देखे / "3 of 16 explored".
    Text
    Only the active reading language, as in What's New (§47): text goes through contentByLang and fonts through titleFontByLang, so gu/kn never render in a Devanagari face. Title 24 (lat 28), description 2 lines in the meaning face.
    Preview
    One of 13 preview kinds: list, checklist, mala, day grid, questions, occasion chips, three steps, three pillars, kundali chart, compass, rashi grid, wish card, widget mock. Each reads from the section's own bundled data and never retypes scripture (RULEBOOK §11.6). Tapping the preview opens the section.
    Actions
    Primary: solid saffron, जप माला खोलें / "Open Japa Mala". Secondary: "Not now" (a11y label Not now, distinct from the update modal's "Later"). ✕, swiping down, tapping the backdrop and Android back all count as Not now.
    Motion
    Backdrop fades in over 200ms and the sheet rises over 280ms. With Reduce Motion on, it appears without animation. No haptic.

    Decisions for you

    1

    Off switch. A row in Settings (recommended), a link inside the sheet, or both.

    Shipping the rating prompt with no off switch is already noted as a store-review risk in RULEBOOK §6.2.

    2

    Measurement. Ship without metrics, or decide separately to log when the sheet is shown and opened.
