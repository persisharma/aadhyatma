⚑ SUPERSEDED — PRD-23 SHIPPED
This prototype was authored 2026-08-22 against main @ 67e22a2, when bhog/naivedya did not exist. PRD-23 has since been built and merged (#301, then #300) and now ships 39 verified profiles covering 68/68 vrat/upavas rules. Canonical references are docs/roadmap/prds/23-bhog-naivedya-vrat-food.md, wiki/subsystems/bhog-naivedya.md, mobile/src/panchang/bhogContent.ts and components/BhogGuidancePanel.tsx — not this file.

The shipped design diverges from this prototype on its central call. Frame A here argues bhog belongs inside the existing "विधि" section ("one home, not two"), which is what created the 8-state matrix warned about in frame C. The shipped build instead gives bhog its own top-level block ("भोग · नैवेद्य · भोजन / Offerings & food") outside the {(upvas || vidhi)} guard — so that guard stays 4-state and the matrix problem never arises. The shipped choice is the simpler one; treat the frames below as a superseded proposal, retained only for its rationale.

Design decisions — frame A

  • One "विधि" home, not two. The shipped screen already composes upvas + vidhi inside a single section ({(upvas || vidhi) && …} in ObservanceDetailScreen.tsx). भोग becomes a third sub-panel inside the same box — not a fourth top-level block. Adding another block header would make the screen read as four competing answers to one question.
  • Ordering is causal, not alphabetical: whether you eat (fast type) → when (window/parana) → what you offer (नैवेद्य) → what is never offered (निषेध) → what you eat (plate) → how you break it. Each row answers the question the previous row raises.
  • निषेध gets its own bordered block because it is the material users cannot reliably find elsewhere — it is the trust-earning half of the feature and must not read as a footnote. Palette is the PRD-14 muted terracotta avoid tone (--avoid), never red: this is "not this", not "danger".
  • Foods are chips, not prose. A permitted/abstained list is scanned in a kitchen, one-handed, mid-cooking — struck-through terracotta chips carry "no" without a second reading. Prose would be read once and forgotten.
  • Salt is a first-class row, not a chip among chips. Sendhā-vs-common salt is the single most-asked fasting question and it is a rule, not an ingredient.
  • पारण भोजन sits directly under the computed parana time from the shipped P4 engine — the two features compose into one answer ("at 5:58 AM, eat this") instead of two disconnected facts.
  • Variance is stated, never resolved. The footnote reuses the upvas strictness voice: sampradāya divergence is named as divergence. The app does not pick a winner between a Smārta and a Vaiṣṇava plate.
All foods, offerings, prohibitions and times here are ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE copy — layout stand-ins, not verified guidance.

Design decisions — frame B

  • No fast means no plate. The permitted/abstained/saltRule group is absent, not rendered empty — same discipline the upvas panel already follows. A festival is not a fast and must not be dressed as one.
  • निषेध survives without a fast. "No tulsī to Gaṇeśa" is the highest-value line on this screen and has nothing to do with fasting — proof that nishedha belongs on the bhog entry, not folded into upvas.
  • The vidhi card keeps its shipped position beneath the facts, inside the same section (PRD-09/P4's settled ordering). भोग slots above it: what you offer is known before the procedure that offers it.
  • This is the common case. 8 of ~162 observance rules carry a vidhiId and 12 carry an upvasId — so for most festivals this panel will be the only thing in the section. It has to stand alone gracefully, which is why it carries its own footnote rather than borrowing the upvas one.
  • Deity-keyed reuse: a Gaṇeśa naivedya entry is shared by every Gaṇeśa observance (Saṅkaṣṭī Caturthī, Gaṇeśa Jayantī, Sakaṭ Cauth) exactly as all Ekādaśīs share one upvas entry — so coverage grows faster than the entry count suggests.

The gate, and the state matrix it creates

  • Draft ≡ absent. getBhogInfo(id) returns null for an unknown id and for status: 'draft' — copied verbatim from getUpvasInfo. Flipping status is a reviewed content change; a green test run never authorizes it.
  • This is why PRD-23 can ship before its content is verified. The registry, the hook, the panel and the tests land OTA and are simply invisible until entries clear review — the pattern PRD-09/P4 established.
  • The real cost, stated plainly: the section guard goes from a 4-state matrix to 8. Every combination needs a designed answer, and three of them are new. This is the one place PRD-23 is more than "copy the upvas pattern".

Design decisions — frame D

  • Two groups, not one merged list. Puja materials and food are bought in different shops and prepared by different people; merging them on the prep screen would help nobody. They merge only on the shopping list (frame E), where a single errand is the point.
  • Checkboxes are the shipped mechanic. data/vidhi/checklistStore.ts already persists samagri ticks per-vidhi-per-festival-date at @vedansh/vidhi-checklist, keyed so last year's list cannot come back pre-ticked. भोग items extend that document — a second store would silently diverge on the date-key rule.
  • निषेध repeats here on purpose. On the prep screen it is actionable ("do not buy tulsī for this") in a way it is not on a reference screen. Same data, different job.
  • "सूची ›" in the top bar is the only new affordance on this screen — it opens frame E. Placed in the header, not as a third button, so the primary action stays "पूजा आरम्भ करें".

Design decisions — frame E

  • Source tags earn their pixels. vidhi / bhog chips tell you which group came from the puja and which from the kitchen — the difference between one shop and two. Without them a merged list is just longer.
  • "न लाएँ" is निषेध re-voiced for the errand. Same entry field, imperative register. A prohibition is only useful at the moment it can still be obeyed, and that moment is in the shop.
  • Date-scoped, and it says so. The footer states the list belongs to this festival date, matching the checklistStore contract. A list that silently persists across years is a bug users cannot see.
  • Share is text, not a card. This one goes to a family WhatsApp thread as a plain checklist someone reads in a shop — the 4:5 parchment share-card family is for verses and reference surfaces, not errands. Deliberate departure; worth pinning in the PRD so it is not "corrected" later.
  • Degrades to vidhi-only. With no verified bhog entry the second group is absent and this is exactly today's samagri list with a header — so the screen is shippable before any bhog content clears review.

What this prototype deliberately does not show

  • No recipes. Ingredients and offerings only. The moment a method step appears, this becomes a cookery app and the verification burden changes completely.
  • No nutrition, no health framing. No calories, no "detox", no benefits copy. Traditional practice as observed.
  • No quantity for the plate. Permitted/abstained is a rule; how much you eat is not the app's business.
  • No single "correct" regional answer. Where practice diverges the entry says so, in the upvas strictness voice.
  • No badge, streak or celebration on any of these surfaces.
