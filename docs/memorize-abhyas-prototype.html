कण्ठस्थ · अभ्यास — Memorization & Recall

PRD-26 (proposed, Q4 round 2) · Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build.
The app has 30+ readers and every one of them only ever shows the text. अभ्यास adds the missing half: a mask over a text you already have, three levels deep, with a private self-marked recall and a spaced review queue. No new screen for the common case — it is a toggle on the reader you are already in (design.md §9). Zero sourced content: every word is already in the binary. Masking is akshara-wise using the repo's hand-rolled SINGLE_AKSHARA matcher, never Intl.Segmenter (ICU 15.1 GB9c splits conjuncts like ज्यो on older runtimes).
1 · The toggle — अभ्यास on the reader you are already in
Reading and practising are the same screen. The toggle sits under the reader header; turning it on masks the page in place and reveals the level bar. Turning it off returns the plain reader — nothing is lost, nothing is a separate "mode" you have to find.

2 · Recall — reveal a line, then mark yourself
At स्तर ३ the verse is gone and only its shape remains. Tap any line to reveal just that line. The self-mark is the whole scoring system: the user says whether it came, and the app believes them. No percentage, no grade, no streak that can be broken.

The due queue — the retention mechanic.
Spaced review puts a legitimately due item in front of the user each morning. This is PRD-11's vow mechanic without an end date: a 41-day sankalp finishes, a chalisa you are learning does not.
3 · Integration, not a silo
RoutineItemKind gains 'memorize' — the same one-line trick PRD-19 used to add 'vidhi' to that union. अभ्यास then lands inside नित्य साधना, Today's Practice, and the shipped completion/celebration path without a parallel bookkeeping system.

Arrival, then maintenance.
Reaching कण्ठस्थ drops the item to a monthly refresh rather than removing it — the practice is keeping it, not getting it. Mirrors the shipped पूर्णाहुति completion-seal pattern (PRD-10/11).
Open questions this prototype does not settle
1. Does the akshara splitter generalise from the repo's SINGLE_AKSHARA thumb matcher to full verse lines? Half-day spike; it decides whether masking is a day or a week.
2. Does mastery state export through PRD-06 backup? Recommended yes — it is the one piece of user data here that takes months to rebuild and cannot be re-derived.
3. gu/kn are runtime transliterations of the Devanagari, so the mask must be computed on the source and transliterated after, or offsets drift. Prototype shows hi only.
4. Review cadence numbers (9 days, 3 weeks above) are placeholders — the schedule needs a pass before it is pinned.
