3 · The morning digest. NEW: identifier daily-muhurat:digest:2026-08-18. One notice folds the whole day in: Abhijit (omitted on the rare day it collapses), the first auspicious choghadiya start, and the Rahu caution — never all eight windows; the tap lands on the full table. This is why there is no "auspicious window starting now" alert: 4–5 pushes/day is the fatigue pattern that gets the whole app's permission revoked, taking the daily verse and japam alarms with it. Night choghadiya are out of alert scope entirely — a midnight-crossing window belongs to no morning plan.

  • The seven shipped families already over-subscribe iOS's shared 64-slot budget in the worst case (wiki notifications, Gotcha #1) — so this family's cap is exactly its arithmetic maximum (DAILY_MUHURAT_CAP = 8), capped soonest-first, and its typical cost is 0 because both toggles ship off.
  • Why only 4 days, not 30: a long horizon exists to cover absent users, but these alerts fire daily — a user absent 4 days hasn't formed the habit they serve, and every foreground re-arms the window. 30 days would burn 60 slots to serve nobody.
  • Why 4 ≤ 7: RULEBOOK §17.6 — any new daily surface must read inside the PREWARM_DAYS = 7 persisted window (panchangDayPrewarm.ts), never sit on its edge. Days 0–4 (day 4 only for day 3's nextSunrise) make every re-arm a hydrate, not fresh astronomy.

Not the muhurat-follow family

  • The shipped muhurat-reminder family alerts on dated one-shots the user followed (occasion + one civil day, graded by verdictForDate, per-follow prefs on the day detail). This phase is recurring daily timing: no follow object, no grading, global prefs in Reminder Settings.
  • Prefix daily-muhurat is deliberately not a string-extension of any shipped prefix — cancels filter with startsWith, so muhurat-reminder-daily would have been silently swept by the follow family. A tsx test pins mutual non-prefixing across all eight families.
  • Same laws as the follow family where they apply: windows are never persisted (sunrise-derived, a stored time lies on city change), and the headless scheduler re-arms on location, calendar-system, foreground, prefs, permission and language — planner pure under tsx --test, glue thin, one Android channel of its own.
