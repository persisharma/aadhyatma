The proposed होम and पाठ surfaces rebuilt in the app's parchment system, token-exact from colors.ts, on Saturday 5 September 2026 in Ujjain, the day and city of the reference screenshots.
New in rev 3 — the real icons. Earlier passes stood the launcher glyphs in as Devanagari letters. Every tile below now draws the app's actual glyph, ported line by line from the StyleSheet in CategoryIcon.tsx: the mala, the diya, the ॐ, the palm-leaf manuscript, the japa counter, the kalash, the shikhara, the temple, the eight-point ashtakam star, the seeing-eye, the ghatika dial. Those icons are React Native View compositions — absolutely positioned boxes with borders, radii and rotations, no SVG — which is why they port to CSS exactly rather than approximately.
Two layout changes still stand from rev 2: the आज का पंचांग strip is kept exactly as shipped and still opens the Panchang tab, and the दैनिक श्लोक card is gone from Home — the daily verse stays on the untouched भक्ति tab. The floating search button stays with it.
Real vs illustrative: every glyph, the panchang headline, the अभिजीत/राहु काल windows, Ganesh Chaturthi on Mon 14 Sep, and every category count come from shipped code. Streak, follow counts and the "जारी रखें" position are illustrative placeholders; the deity rail uses letter discs where the app draws its 21 hand-made DeityIcon glyphs.

Constraints the prototype honours

  • No new icon grammar. Fifteen glyphs are the shipped CategoryIcon drawings, reused at their existing sizes. वास्तु is the one new drawing and follows the same rules — View strokes, saffron-deep on gold accent, no SVG and no emoji (design.md §5/§42).
  • First frame is the final frame (§64). The Today strip is untouched, so this was already true; the साधना row and उपकरण row must reserve their height the same way.
  • Festival first. The toran, the leading आज के लिए card, the strip's chips and the morning notification all read the same catalog, so they keep naming the same day.
  • भक्ति stays untouched. The daily-verse pool selector, the widget deep link, the reminder and design.md §23 are exactly as shipped.
