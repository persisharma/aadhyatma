वेदांश़ Today-First Home

The proposed होम, पाठ, पंचांग and अन्य surfaces rebuilt in the app's parchment system, token-exact from colors.ts, on Saturday 5 September 2026 in Ujjain, the day and city of the reference screenshots.
What the prototype claims: Home answers "what does today ask of me" first, then "what do I do daily", then "what do I want to do", and the text library moves one level down into its own tab. Every engine and component shown already ships: the Today strip, the आज का विधान briefing, the Muhurat glance card's live kicker, the per-date verse selector the widget uses, the routine and sankalp trackers, the observance catalog.
Real vs illustrative: the panchang headline, राहु काल and अभिजीत windows, the running उद्वेग chaughadiya, Gita 2.47 and its meaning, Ganesh Chaturthi on Mon 14 Sep, and every category count come from the shipped data. Tithi end times, the nakshatra, the rashifal line, streak and follow counts, and the "जारी रखें" position are illustrative placeholders.

Constraints the prototype honours

  • First frame is the final frame (§64). The आज card reserves its full height before the panchang solve lands; the headline paints from the cached day store exactly as the Today strip does now. Nothing above the उपकरण grid may grow after the splash lifts.
  • The Ask engine stays off the launch graph. The briefing rows inside the आज card load behind the same dynamic import आज का विधान uses; the panchang headline and windows do not depend on it.
  • Festival first. The toran, the leading आज के लिए card and the morning notification keep naming the same festival; the आज card's व्रत-पर्व row reads the same rule.
  • One daily verse. Home, the widget and the reminder all use the per-date pool index, so a reminder tap lands on the verse it promised.
  • Phase 2 touches contracts: the daily-verse cold-start target, tour steps 2 and 12–13, the Maestro flows that tap tab-bhakti, and design.md §17, §18, §23, §32, §48, §50, §71.
