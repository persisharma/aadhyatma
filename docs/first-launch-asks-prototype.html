“Keep asking until yes”: what's possible

Hard limit

iPhone shows its system prompt once, ever. After “Don’t Allow” the app can’t show it again; only the Settings app can turn notifications on.

Hard limit

Android 13+ shows it at most twice. After the second refusal, Settings only. Android 12 and older have no prompt; notifications start on.

Our side

Our own reminder screen can come back as often as we decide. Before a refusal it leads to the system prompt; after one it becomes a “turn on in Settings” screen with a button straight to Vedansh’s settings.

Cadence

At most once per day the app is opened, until “हाँ”. After five “बाद में”, once a week. Only at launch, never mid-reading. Exact numbers are a product call.

Risk

Apple doesn’t allow an app to require notifications, so the screen must always be dismissable. Daily asks to someone who keeps declining can cost uninstalls and reviews.

Sunrise times come from the app’s own engine for 30 Oct 2026. The Panchang widget’s tithi and “तक” time are example values. System prompts use each OS’s wording; the location reason is the app’s real permission text.
