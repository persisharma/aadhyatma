The seventh vidhi, anchored to a personal tithi instead of a festival — and the PRD-17 Pitru Smaran Phase-3 cross-link. Plus the Part-B recorded-audio affordance and Part-C variant selection, both annotated as options.

How to read this page: each phone is one shipped-pattern screen; numbered gold dots match the numbered notes beside it. Muted gold-and-ink tone throughout — this flow deliberately carries Pitru Smaran's reverent register, never the festive saffron celebration accents.

⚠ EVERY LITURGICAL AND PROCEDURAL STRING BELOW IS AN ILLUSTRATIVE PLACEHOLDER — NONE OF IT IS SOURCED CONTENT.
Real shraddha/tarpan content is blocked on source verification: RULEBOOK §11.3 forbids authoring liturgy or claiming unopened sources, and PRD-19 §3.4's two-source verification cannot run from this authoring environment — DrikPanchang and archive.org were unreachable on 2026-08-12 and 2026-08-14 (recorded in each shipped entry's canonicalEditionStatus). Samagri items, step titles, instructions and sequence shown here exist only to demonstrate layout and interaction. Nothing on this page may be transcribed into mobile/src/data/vidhi/. Privacy contract (side annotation, applies to every phone above)
· Vidhi state (@vedansh/vidhi-checklist) is keyed by vidhi + dateKey (+ variantId) — never by person.
· Vidhi route params: {vidhiId, dateMs} only.
· No new notifications; the shipped per-person smaran reminders remain the only fires and never change copy.
· Nothing from this flow reaches any share card; the samagri text share carries items only.
Content gates (side annotation)
· Two-source verification (§3.4) per vidhi and per variant; Gita Press canonical edition recorded verified or honestly pending.
· Mantra steps ship verbatim-transcribed or instruction-only — never approximated (§11.3).
· Vidhi absent from the registry ⇒ every door on this page absent. No teaser states exist.
· The 2026-08-12 / 2026-08-14 egress attempts are the recorded precedent; authoring waits for an egress-capable environment.
