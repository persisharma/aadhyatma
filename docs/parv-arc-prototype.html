PRD-28 (proposed, Q4 round 2) · Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build.
data/vidhi/ ships ganesh-chaturthi-sthapana and navratri-ghatasthapana and no visarjan of anything — the app knows how to install a deity and has never once concluded a rite. festivals.ts carries Diwali's five days as four unrelated rules and ganesh-chaturthi/anant-chaturdashi as strangers. This adds an arc relation over the rules that already exist (arcId, arcRole, ordinal — additive, no rule rewritten, no date changed) plus the solver that turns the family's own duration choice into their visarjan date. The only date-critical item in the slate: Diwali falls inside Q4.
1 · The choice the app has never asked for
Ganesh Chaturthi is not a day, it is a household commitment of 1½, 3, 5, 7 or 10 days — and the concluding date depends on that choice, which is exactly why it cannot be a static calendar entry. The chooser appears at स्थापना, once.

Navratri — where the arc pays off twice.
The arc knows tomorrow is Kanya Pujan, so PRD-23's shipped bhog list and grocery checklist surface a day early, when the shopping actually happens. New value from content already in the binary.
Open questions this prototype does not settle
1. Which durations ship (1½ / 3 / 5 / 7 / 10) and does any default? Recommended: offer the set, default to nothing — regional and household variance is wide and a default reads as a prescription.
2. What happens when a user never chooses? The arc must degrade to today's behaviour (independent days), never nag.
3. Visarjan vidhi text is sourced content and ships verified or invisible — the arc, solver and strip must be useful without it, which is why they are Phase A.
4. Do Navratri and Diwali arcs re-use the same arcRole vocabulary, or does Diwali need a fifth role for a day that is neither sthapana nor visarjan? Prototype assumes 'day' covers it.
