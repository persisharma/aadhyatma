PRD-46 · Interactive UI prototype. Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build. Art placeholders stand in for the shipped ComicArt / KidsStoryArt renders.
A quiz that is a door back into the text. Every question anchors to a shipped artifact (comic panel · story page · shloka) and every answer — right or wrong — ends at the source with a one-tap पढ़ें › hand-off into the real reader route (ComicReader/KidsStoryReader/Gita). No timers, no lives, no leaderboards, no child profiles. Packs list on the shipped §82 ListingCard; entry sits in the Kids library beside the comic.

3 · गीता परीक्षा — the formal exam mode (Phase 2)
Modeled on India's school Gita parikshas: a level ladder taken in succession, a declared adhyaya syllabus, a fixed deterministic paper (no hints, no reveals mid-paper), ~50% pass with graded bands, and a certificate for every child — pass or attempt.
