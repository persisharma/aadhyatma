गृह वास्तु · मेरा घर — three UX variants

PRD-24 Phase 2 · how a buyer, a renter, or a householder marks a home room by room and reads it against the weighted convention. Every frame below is live: turn the compass, place rooms, watch the findings recompute.

Shared across all three variants — the five finding classes from PRD §0.2 (निषिद्ध स्थान · विधान से भिन्न · श्रेयस् अनुपलब्ध · परंपरागत विकल्प · मेल), counts per class and never a total, registry order within a group, the accommodation line under every forbidden/differs row, and the privacy line. What differs is how the position is captured and how the assessment is read.

Registry rows, pada names and weights in this prototype are illustrative placeholders pending the two-domain verification RULEBOOK §22.3 requires. Rooms shown in Hindi as the app renders them; English in italics is the prototype's own caption.

Variant A चक्कर · the compass walk
Chosen · 5 Sep

Stand at the door, then at the centre — point and tap

Sensor-first, for a site visit. Step 1 captures the facing (with the pada ring, since it is a door). Step 2 walks the template's rooms as a checklist: point the phone at a room, tap its chip, the faced dik is recorded. Step 3 is the assessment as a mandala with your rooms in it. Drag the slider to turn the compass — it stands in for the phone rotating.

Variant B मंडल पर रखें · place on the mandala
Ledger reading dropped · compare kept

No compass needed — put each room in its cell

For the person with a brochure plan in hand, or comparing listings from the sofa. Pick the facing, tap a room chip, tap the cell it sits in. The assessment reads as a ledger sorted by class with a stripe per row, and the third frame compares two shortlisted homes side by side. Tap a chip, then a cell.

21:10◔ ▲ ▮

Placement. The door glyph moves to the facing edge. A selected chip names the room to place; placing tints nothing yet — evaluation waits for the assessment screen, so placement never feels like being marked.

21:14◔ ▲ ▮

Ledger assessment. Same engine, different reading order: the strip pinned at top, a mini-mandala beside the title, then one row per room with a class stripe on the left and the accommodation under it. Faster to scan on a phone than the grid; less spatial.

21:20◔ ▲ ▮

Compare. Two considering homes, columns per home, class counts then every room's zone and class. No winner, no colour beyond the class tokens. The left column follows your placements in the first frame; the right is a saved sample.

Variant C नक्शे पर · mark the floor plan
Chosen · grid view

Upload the plan, set north, pin the rooms

Part F. Frame 1 is F1 — offline, deterministic: rotate the north arrow to match the brochure, tap a chip then tap the plan; zone comes from the angle around the centre pin. Frame 2 reads the assessment on the plan itself. Frame 3 is F2 — the AI pre-read: pins arrive unconfirmed, every one needs a tap, and the pointers cite finding ids or are dropped. Turn the north slider and watch zones change.

Decision (5 Sep 2026). Variant A is the capture flow and Variant C the floor-plan input; the mandala grid is the one assessment reading — Variant B's ledger is dropped, so the assessment screen has a single layout and C's grid toggle becomes its default. B's compare screen stays: it belongs to the roster, not to how one home is read.
