Journey contract (§2.7 / §4a) — as coded

  • Implemented as DaanJourneyScreen {occasionId}, registered on the More and Panchang stacks (multi-stack door pattern) so Observance Detail pushes in place.
  • Step 3 links this occasion's katha: shipped kathas open the existing VratKathaReader; new teaching-kathas open DaanKathaScreen (Sankranti links Karna — Sūryaputra on the Sūrya parva).

Rule-id mapping (as coded in occasions.ts)

  • Exact ids win over family suffixes: shattila-ekadashi gets its til row; every other *-ekadashi falls to the shared parana-anna row; makar-sankranti beats the shared *-sankranti snāna-dāna row.
  • Tier 1 ids: makar-sankranti, akshaya-tritiya, shraddha-dates, akshaya-navami, guru-purnima, vasant-panchami, ganga-dussehra, ganga-saptami, govardhan-puja, bachh-baras, dhanteras, diwali, karthigai-vrat, chhath-puja — every one verified against the shipped solver tables.
  • Tier 2: amavasya-vrat (somvati elevation stated in-row), purnima-vrat + shree-satyanarayan-vrat, the ekadashi family.
  • Tier 4 journeys ride Pitru Smaran (privacy stance intact) and the Kundali janma-tithi.
  • The Observance Detail door renders only when getDaanOccasionForRule(ruleId) matches — absent otherwise, never a placeholder.
