# Rishis and knowledge traditions

The opening article should distinguish a sage in a narrative, a traditionally attributed seer, a teacher and a deity. A named sage's literary appearance is not, on its own, a modern biographical date.

## Saptarshi in a defined context

Bhagavata Purana 8.13.5 names **Kashyapa, Atri, Vasistha, Vishvamitra, Gautama, Jamadagni and Bharadvaja**. The chapter frames them within the present Manu's period; other manvantara rosters need their own references. [SB8135-BBT](sources.md#sb8135-bbt).

**Hindi draft:** सप्तऋषि का परिचय देते समय चुने गए ग्रंथ और मन्वन्तर का संदर्भ रखें। इस सूची में सात ऋषि हैं, लेकिन हर ग्रंथ या काल-संदर्भ में यही सात नाम होना आवश्यक नहीं है।

**English draft:** Introduce a Saptarshi list with its text and manvantara context. This passage names seven sages; it does not make every different roster erroneous.

## Seven sourced profile seeds

| कश्यप / Kashyapa | भागवत के वामन-प्राकट्य प्रसंग में कश्यप और अदिति का उल्लेख मिलता है। | Kashyapa and Aditi appear in the Bhagavata account of Vamana's manifestation. | [SB818-BBT](sources.md#sb818-bbt), 8.18 summary/index. Do not extrapolate a full genealogy of every creature. |
| अत्रि / Atri | रामायण में अत्रि राम, सीता और लक्ष्मण का स्वागत करते हैं और अनसूया का परिचय आता है। | Atri receives Rama, Sita and Lakshmana; Anasuya is introduced in the same episode. | [VR-ATRI](sources.md#vr-atri), Ayodhya 2.117.5–8. |
| वसिष्ठ / Vasistha | रामायण के शबला-प्रसंग में वसिष्ठ के आश्रम का वर्णन है। | Vasistha's hermitage appears in the Shabala episode. | [VR-VASISHTHA](sources.md#vr-vasishtha), Bala 1.53. |
| विश्वामित्र / Vishvamitra | विश्वामित्र रामायण की यात्रा और ऋषि-कथाओं में प्रमुख हैं। | Vishvamitra is prominent in the Ramayana's journey and sage narratives. | Bala 1.48 narrator and 1.53 episode. Do not retell his entire transformation from one chapter. |
| गौतम / Gautama | रामायण में गौतम का नाम अहल्या के प्रसंग में आता है। | Gautama appears in the Ahalya episode of the Ramayana. | [VR-GAUTAMA](sources.md#vr-gautama), Bala 1.48. Later stone-transformation retellings must not be silently substituted into this passage. |
| जमदग्नि / Jamadagni | भागवत में जमदग्नि परशुराम के पिता के रूप में परिचित हैं। | Jamadagni is introduced as Parashurama's father in the Bhagavata. | [SB915-BBT](sources.md#sb915-bbt), 9.15 summary/index. Story violence is not an instruction about modern social groups. |
| भरद्वाज / Bharadvaja | प्रयाग के आश्रम में भरद्वाज राम को चित्रकूट का मार्ग बताते हैं। | At the Prayaga hermitage, Bharadvaja advises Rama about Chitrakuta. | [VR-BHARADVAJA](sources.md#vr-bharadvaja), Ayodhya 2.54. |

These are limited identity seeds, not seven fully collated lives. The Valmiki online text's Sanskrit, translation and commentary must be separated; Sanskrit Documents mirrors do not create another independent edition.

## Knowledge transmission

A reader can meet a tradition through questions, dialogue, recitation, commentary and teaching lineages. Teach those modes through the actual passages above. Do not promise lineage authentication from surname, gotra or a quiz result. A complete gotra catalogue and a map of philosophical schools would be separate research projects.

## Review still needed

Independent editions for the short profiles; careful treatment of Ahalya and violent episodes; Sanskrit name normalization; and a specialist-reviewed account of the distinction between rishi, muni, acharya and guru before those become standalone dictionary definitions. Women questioners and teachers should remain visible in the learning map, rather than being hidden behind one seven-member roster.
