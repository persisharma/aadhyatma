# Panchang and sacred time

A terminology dossier. No current festival dates, auspicious-time recommendations or new calculation engine are supplied.

## Five limbs

IMD's Positional Astronomy Centre explains the Rashtriya Panchanga and its astronomical elements. The following are concise definitions, not instructions to reproduce its calculations. [IMD-PANCHANG](sources.md#imd-panchang).

| Term | Hindi draft | English draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| वार / Vara | सप्ताह का दिन। | Day of the week. |
| तिथि / Tithi | सूर्य और चन्द्रमा के भूकेन्द्रीय देशान्तर के अन्तर में प्रत्येक 12° की वृद्धि से जुड़ा विभाग। | Each 12° division of geocentric Moon–Sun longitude difference. |
| नक्षत्र / Nakshatra | चन्द्रमा के निरयण देशान्तर के 27 विभागों में से एक, प्रत्येक 13°20′ का। | One of 27 divisions of the Moon's sidereal longitude, each 13°20′. |
| योग / Yoga | सूर्य और चन्द्रमा के निरयण देशान्तरों के योग के 27 विभागों में से एक। | One of 27 divisions of the sum of sidereal Sun and Moon longitudes. |
| करण / Karana | तिथि का आधा भाग; देशान्तर-अन्तर का 6° विभाग। | Half a tithi: a 6° elongation division. |

These angular intervals do not have a fixed civil-clock duration. A nakshatra division is not exactly the same object as a modern astronomical constellation. This calendrical yoga is not a physical exercise routine or every astrological yoga combination.

| कृष्ण पक्ष / Krishna paksha | पूर्णिमा से अमावस्या की ओर का पक्ष। The waning half of a lunar month. |

| अमावस्या / Amavasya | नए चन्द्र से जुड़ी तिथि। New-moon tithi; distinguish the day label from the conjunction instant. |

| अमान्त / Amanta | अमावस्या पर समाप्त होने वाले चान्द्र मास की पद्धति। A lunar-month convention ending at new moon. |
| पूर्णिमान्त / Purnimanta | पूर्णिमा पर समाप्त होने वाले चान्द्र मास की पद्धति। A lunar-month convention ending at full moon. |
| अधिक मास / Adhika masa | संबंधित चान्द्र मास में संक्रान्ति न होने पर आने वाला अतिरिक्त मास। An intercalary lunar month with no solar ingress in the relevant lunation. |

Month conventions: [DRIK-MONTHS](sources.md#drik-months). Extra month and ingress explanation: [DRIK-ADHIK](sources.md#drik-adhik). IMD provides the underlying astronomical terminology. The position of an extra month is calculated; it is not random and not exactly once every three calendar years. ## Reading a time label

Drik's FAQ says its day Panchang lists **ending** times. An entry such as “Chaturdashi until 14:15” says when that tithi finishes, not when it starts. This is that publisher's convention; the FAQ itself notes that other almanacs sometimes mix end and start conventions. Times are displayed for the selected location/time zone. [DRIK-TIMINGS](sources.md#drik-timings).

**Hindi draft:** पंचांग में समय पढ़ने से पहले देखें कि वह आरम्भ है या समाप्ति। तिथि का समय घड़ी के 24 घंटे का स्थिर दिन नहीं है। सही स्थान और समय-क्षेत्र भी चुनें।

**English draft:** Before reading a Panchang time, check whether it marks a beginning or an ending. A tithi is not a fixed 24-hour civil day. Use the correct location and time zone.

If the future reader uses times beyond 24:00, explain that display convention explicitly with an actual verified example from that reader. This research does not establish a universal 24+ notation rule for all almanacs.

## Why locations or calendars can differ

A geocentric angular transition is one absolute instant in a given computational system. Local clock conversion and sunrise differ by place. Festival observance may additionally depend on a tithi's overlap with sunrise, moonrise or another prescribed interval, the month convention and the tradition followed. Do not say the geocentric tithi transition itself changes merely because two people are in different cities.

Sources: IMD's astronomical basis and Drik's location/timing/month explanations. Exact festival-decision rules, ayanamsha choices, ephemerides and observational sunrise conventions require their own implementation audit.

## Religious time and astronomy

**Hindi:** एक पंचांग में खगोलीय गणना और धार्मिक आचरण के नियम दोनों काम कर सकते हैं। इनके प्रश्न अलग हैं: आकाशीय स्थिति कब बदलती है, और किसी परम्परा में उत्सव किस दिन मनाया जाता है।

**English:** Astronomical calculation and religious observance rules answer different questions: when a celestial relationship changes, and when a community observes a festival.

Rahu and Ketu are covered in [Navagraha](05-deities-and-navagraha.md#navagraha-reference-roster). For eclipse geometry use NASA's orbital-node explanation; do not present the devotional narrative as a competing scientific calculation.
