# Avataras, Navdurga and Ashtalakshmi

Original short-copy drafts and reference rosters. Membership and iconography are separate editorial decisions.

## Avataras: opening explanation

**Hindi:** अवतार की धारणा विशेष रूप से वैष्णव परम्पराओं में महत्वपूर्ण है। गीता में धर्म की हानि के समय दिव्य प्राकट्य की चर्चा आती है। दशावतार एक प्रसिद्ध चयन है; भागवत में इससे बड़ी सूची और असंख्य अवतारों का कथन भी मिलता है।

**English:** Avatara is especially important in Vaishnava traditions. The Gita describes divine manifestation in relation to dharma. Dashavatara is a familiar selection, while the Bhagavata presents a longer enumeration and speaks of innumerable avataras.

References: Gita 4.7–8 context, [BG47-MUKUND](sources.md#bg47-mukund), [BG47-BBT](sources.md#bg47-bbt); Bhagavata 1.3, [SB13-BBT](sources.md#sb13-bbt), [SB1326-BBT](sources.md#sb1326-bbt). Different schools describe Krishna's relationship to Vishnu differently.

| Contemporary Drik list | Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama, Balarama, Krishna, Kalki | [DRIK-DASHAVATARA](sources.md#drik-dashavatara) |
| Jayadeva's opening Gita Govinda hymn | Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Parashurama, Rama, Balarama/Haladhara, Buddha, Kalki | [JAYADEVA](sources.md#jayadeva) |

In Jayadeva's hymn Krishna/Keshava is the addressee; Buddha and Haladhara both appear among the ten forms. Do not replace one with Krishna while calling the result Jayadeva's list. A commonly circulated list containing Krishna and Buddha is another candidate lens; this pack does not assign it to a specific unchecked passage.

## Navdurga

### What is firmly located

Devi Kavacha 3–5 supplies the familiar nine names in sequence. It is a recitation adjunct associated with the Devi Mahatmya tradition, distinct from the thirteen-chapter narrative. [DEVI-KAVACHA](sources.md#devi-kavacha). Navratri observance and divine-form lists have regional variants; [DRIK-NAVRATRI](sources.md#drik-navratri).

**Hindi:** नवदुर्गा के इस परिचय में देवी-कवच के नौ नाम लिए गए हैं। प्रत्येक स्वरूप का चित्र और पूजा-संदर्भ संबंधित परम्परा के अनुसार समझें। इन्हें देवी के नौ क्रमिक जन्मों की एक ही जीवनी मानकर न पढ़ें।

**English:** This introduction follows the nine names in the Devi Kavacha. Images and observance details need their own tradition labels. The sequence should not be turned into a biography of nine consecutive births.

## Ashtalakshmi

The checked stotra has eight named sections. The names describe dimensions of auspicious abundance; they do not promise eight measurable outcomes.

| Stotra form | Hindi editorial gloss | English editorial gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| आदि लक्ष्मी / Adi | आद्य स्वरूप | Primordial form |
| धान्य लक्ष्मी / Dhanya | अन्न और पोषण | Grain and nourishment |
| धैर्य लक्ष्मी / Dhairya | धैर्य | Courage and steadiness |
| गज लक्ष्मी / Gaja | गजों सहित स्वरूप | Form with elephants |
| सन्तान लक्ष्मी / Santana | सन्तति का विषय | Continuity and offspring |
| विजय लक्ष्मी / Vijaya | विजय | Victory |
| विद्या लक्ष्मी / Vidya | विद्या | Learning |
| धन लक्ष्मी / Dhana | धन-सम्पदा | Material abundance |

Source: [ASHTALAKSHMI-STOTRA](sources.md#ashtalakshmi-stotra), eight headed sections. These short glosses are editorial aids, not a translation of the entire hymn. Authorship, date and ancient-text attribution remain unestablished.

The Sri Ashtalakshmi Temple's own About page instead lists **Maha, Dhanya, Dhairya, Rajya, Santhana, Jaya, Veera and Dhana**. Keep that complete local list under the temple's name. Do not turn Rajya into Gaja or remove Veera merely to match the stotra. [ASHTALAKSHMI-TEMPLE](sources.md#ashtalakshmi-temple).

**Hindi:** अष्टलक्ष्मी में समृद्धि के अनेक आयामों का स्मरण किया जाता है। स्तोत्र और मंदिर की सूची में नाम बदल सकते हैं। इस पाठ में चुनी गई सूची का स्रोत साथ दिया जाएगा।

**English:** Ashtalakshmi honours several dimensions of prosperity. Stotra and temple lists can differ; the selected enumeration should always carry its source.
