# Sacred symbols and worship objects

Status: sourced research notes and original bilingual copy drafts. Every entry remains an editorial draft.

## Swastika

**Core:** an auspicious Indic sign used in household, business and worship settings. HAF documents several interpretations of its limbs; these are parallel symbolic readings, not proof of one original construction rule. ISKCON Educational Services also places it among auspicious Hindu symbols.

**Hindi draft:** स्वस्तिक भारतीय धार्मिक परम्पराओं में मंगल का चिह्न है। पूजा और शुभ अवसरों पर इसके उपयोग तथा इसकी चार भुजाओं की व्याख्या अलग संदर्भों में मिलती है। किसी एक व्याख्या को सभी परम्पराओं का एकमात्र अर्थ न मानें।

**English draft:** The swastika is used as an auspicious sign in Indian religious traditions. Its forms, settings and symbolic interpretations vary; one reading need not explain every use.

**Deepening:** meanings of svasti; documented uses; dots and regional forms; differences between a sacred drawing and later political appropriation if a user asks. Do not make angle or rotation a sufficient historical distinction. No universal “wrong direction brings misfortune” claim.

Sources: [HAF-SWASTIKA](sources.md#haf-swastika), [HOH-SYMBOLS](sources.md#hoh-symbols). A complete scholarly history and drawing-convention comparison still needs separate research.

## Om

**Core:** Mandukya relates Om to the Self and the waking, dream and deep-sleep states, with a fourth beyond those divisions. Read the twelve verses rather than treating a modern graphic explanation as the entire text.

**Hindi draft:** ॐ को प्रणव भी कहा जाता है। माण्डूक्य उपनिषद् में इसके माध्यम से आत्मा और चेतना की अवस्थाओं पर विचार किया गया है। अन्य पूजा-परम्पराओं में इसकी व्याख्या और उपयोग भी मिलते हैं।

**English draft:** Om, also called pranava, is explored in the Mandukya Upanishad through the Self and states of awareness. Its other devotional uses should be explained in their own context.

Sources: [MANDUKYA-MI](sources.md#mandukya-mi), [HOH-SYMBOLS](sources.md#hoh-symbols). The journal article is credited to Adhyeta (March 1957, pp 13–18); its twelve English passages were located. The Gateway page is a navigation aid, not a second collated edition. Do not claim a medically established frequency or that a sound recording cures disease.

## Shri

**Core:** Shri is associated with auspiciousness and Lakshmi; it can also function as a respectful word before a name. The Shri Sukta invokes Shri/Lakshmi and contains lotus imagery.

**Hindi draft:** श्री शुभता और लक्ष्मी से जुड़ा शब्द है। इसका उपयोग सम्मानसूचक संबोधन और मंगल-लेखन में भी होता है। श्रीसूक्त में श्री को देवी के रूप में संबोधित किया गया है।

**English draft:** Shri is associated with auspiciousness and Lakshmi and is also used as an honorific. The Shri Sukta addresses Shri as a goddess.

Sources: [SHRI-SUKTA](sources.md#shri-sukta), [HOH-LAKSHMI](sources.md#hoh-lakshmi). Do not equate every calligraphic Shri with a consecrated Shri Chakra.

## Kalasha

**Core:** a ritual pot, often with water, leaves and coconut. Drik documents a specific installation procedure. Chinmaya and Sri Deva Sthanam give distinct symbolic interpretations.

**Hindi draft:** कलश पूजा में प्रयुक्त पात्र है। कई विधियों में इसमें जल रखकर पत्ते और नारियल सजाए जाते हैं। इसके रूप और प्रतीकात्मक अर्थ संबंधित पूजा और परम्परा के अनुसार समझने चाहिए।

**English draft:** A kalasha is a ritual vessel, often arranged with water, leaves and coconut. Its preparation and meanings depend on the ceremony and tradition.

Sources: [DRIK-KALASH](sources.md#drik-kalash), [DRIK-GHATA](sources.md#drik-ghata), [SDS-RITUALS](sources.md#sds-rituals). Do not turn one Navratri preparation into an invariant rule for every kalasha.

## Coconut

**Core:** used above a kalasha and in other offerings. Contemporary explanations may compare the shell and inner fruit to ego and inner life; label that interpretation.

**Hindi draft:** नारियल कई पूजाओं में अर्पित किया जाता है और कलश के ऊपर भी रखा जाता है। उसके खोल और भीतर के फल से जुड़ी आध्यात्मिक उपमाएँ मिलती हैं। ये उपमाएँ और विधियाँ परम्परा के अनुसार बदल सकती हैं।

**English draft:** Coconut appears in offerings and kalasha arrangements. Some teachers use its shell and inner fruit as a spiritual metaphor; that explanation should be attributed.

Sources: [SDS-RITUALS](sources.md#sds-rituals), [DRIK-GHATA](sources.md#drik-ghata). Do not label a modern ego analogy as a quotation from an unspecified Veda.

## Lamp

**Core:** light is offered in worship; teachers relate it to knowledge and the removal of ignorance. Different lamps and arrangements have distinct contexts.

**Hindi draft:** दीपक पूजा में प्रकाश अर्पित करने का माध्यम है। आध्यात्मिक व्याख्याओं में इसका प्रकाश ज्ञान और अज्ञान के हटने की उपमा बनता है। दीपों की संख्या और विधि पूजा के अनुसार बदलती है।

**English draft:** The lamp offers light in worship. Teachers often use its light as a metaphor for knowledge; arrangements and ritual details vary.

Sources: [CHINMAYA-WHY](sources.md#chinmaya-why), [SDS-RITUALS](sources.md#sds-rituals). No universal oil choice, fixed wick count or promise that a lamp changes a household's finances.

## Conch

**Core:** a recognizable Vishnu attribute and an instrument used in some worship. Keep the held attribute, named epic conches and the practical blowing instrument distinct.

**Hindi draft:** शंख विष्णु के प्रमुख आयुधों में दिखाई देता है और कुछ पूजा-विधियों में बजाया जाता है। देव-चित्र में धारण किया शंख और पूजा में प्रयुक्त शंख संबंधित संदर्भों में समझें।

**English draft:** The conch is a recognizable attribute of Vishnu and is sounded in some worship traditions. Its iconographic and practical uses need separate explanation.

Sources: [MET-RECOGNIZING](sources.md#met-recognizing), [HOH-SYMBOLS](sources.md#hoh-symbols). Claims about destroying microbes or curing respiratory disease are excluded. Shankha species, chirality and handling rules need separate sources before advice.

## Bell

**Core:** ritual bell sounding is documented in Kamika; Chinmaya explains the sound through attention and auspiciousness.

**Hindi draft:** घंटी का नाद पूजा का हिस्सा है। कई शिक्षक इसे मन को पूजा की ओर लौटाने और मंगल का स्मरण कराने से जोड़ते हैं। मंदिर में इसका उपयोग वहाँ की व्यवस्था के अनुसार करें।

**English draft:** Bell sounding is part of worship. Some teachers explain it as a way to gather attention and invoke auspiciousness.

Sources: [KAMIKA](sources.md#kamika), [CHINMAYA-WHY](sources.md#chinmaya-why). Do not claim a universal seven-second brain effect, a precise healing frequency or that the deity must be awakened by noise.

## Lotus

**Core:** a devotional and artistic motif; Gita 5.10 uses the lotus leaf as a simile for action without attachment. Museum objects document lotus seats and held flowers.

**Hindi draft:** कमल देव-चित्रों और धार्मिक साहित्य में अनेक रूपों में आता है। गीता 5.10 में कमल-पत्र की उपमा कर्म करते हुए आसक्ति से मुक्त रहने को समझाती है। हर कमल-चित्र का अर्थ केवल इसी श्लोक से तय नहीं होता।

**English draft:** The lotus appears in religious art and literature. Gita 5.10 uses its leaf as a simile for acting without attachment; other lotus images have their own contexts.

Sources: [BG510-MUKUND](sources.md#bg510-mukund), [BG510-BBT](sources.md#bg510-bbt), [MET-SARASVATI](sources.md#met-sarasvati).

## Tilaka

**Core:** forehead marks vary by lineage, occasion and individual practice. BAPS explains its own tilak-chandlo in terms of devotion and service; that explanation is not universal.

**Hindi draft:** तिलक की आकृति, सामग्री और अर्थ सम्प्रदाय तथा अवसर के अनुसार बदलते हैं। किसी विशेष परम्परा के चिह्न को समझने के लिए उसी परम्परा की व्याख्या पढ़ें।

**English draft:** Tilaka forms and meanings vary by community and occasion. A particular lineage's mark should be explained through that lineage's account.

Sources: [BAPS-TILAK](sources.md#baps-tilak), [SDS-TILAK](sources.md#sds-tilak). Do not infer a person's character or prescribe one mark for everyone.

## Mauli and Kalava

**Core:** thread appears in ritual arrangements and protective/vow practices. Rakhi, wrist mauli and the thread around a kalasha should not be treated as identical ceremonies.

**Hindi draft:** मौली या कलावा कई पूजा-परम्पराओं में उपयोग होता है। कहीं यह संकल्प और मंगल का स्मरण है, तो कहीं रक्षा-प्रार्थना का हिस्सा। कलाई और कलश पर बाँधे जाने वाले धागे की विधि एक जैसी मानकर न चलें।

**English draft:** Sacred thread appears in several worship and vow practices. A wrist thread, rakhi and a kalasha thread should be explained within their respective ceremonies.

Sources: [DRIK-KALASH](sources.md#drik-kalash), [HOH-RAKSHA](sources.md#hoh-raksha). A universal wrist, knot count, removal day or historical origin is not established by these sources.

## Akshata

**Core:** unbroken rice grains used in the checked Ghatasthapana procedure. Chinmaya offers an interpretation connected with the imperishable; distinguish that teaching from the material definition.

**Hindi draft:** अक्षत सामान्यतः बिना टूटे चावल के दानों को कहा जाता है, जिनका उपयोग कई पूजा-विधियों में होता है। इनके रंग, मिश्रण और अर्पण का तरीका संबंधित पूजा के अनुसार देखें।

**English draft:** Akshata are unbroken rice grains used in several worship procedures. Their preparation and offering depend on the ceremony.

Sources: [DRIK-GHATA](sources.md#drik-ghata), [CHINMAYA-WHY](sources.md#chinmaya-why). Do not invent a universal energy mechanism or say all deities accept the same offering.

## Source independence and use

Drik's two procedure pages are one publisher, not two independent confirmations of an interpretation. Multiple reproductions of Chinmaya's Why Do We are one explanatory tradition. These notes provide traceable research; every exact liturgical quotation still needs full collation and the repository's publication gate.
