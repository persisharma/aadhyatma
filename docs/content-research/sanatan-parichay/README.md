This pack gathers the section's content foundation for later authoring and integration. It contains **13 research/editorial dossiers**, a **201-topic inventory**, **107 source records**, **44 FAQ drafts**, original Hindi/English introductions and a review backlog. ## Start here

1. Read the relevant subject dossier below.
2. Follow its stable source IDs into [sources.md](sources.md), or inspect [sources.json](sources.json).

5. Use the [FAQ](faq.md) for bounded answers and cross-links.

## Evidence depth

- **18 draft summaries:** the six foundations and twelve core symbols have original short Hindi and English copy.
- **169 reference seeds:** a definition, identity, synopsis or source location exists; a full article may still need authoring and collation.
- **14 follow-ups:** a deeper source, disputed mapping or dedicated dossier is still required.
- **No unit is publication-cleared.** All remain draft.

Of the 107 source records, 103 have page/excerpt reading, two are inaccessible and two are gateway/reader-shell records. Access alone is not an authority grade. The Toran page has a quality concern; the Kushmanda page has an identity error. Mirrors of one edition and multiple pages by one publisher are not independent corroboration.

## Findings that change the content

- The chosen thirty-three-deity passage and later named rosters must stay separate.
- Koti cannot be settled by the slogan “types, never crore.”
- The downward guardian and several deity depictions vary by text.
- Dashavatara and Ashtalakshmi need declared lists.
- Navdurga names belong to the checked Kavacha reference; do not manufacture nine sequential births.
- Gayatri/Savitr identity needs review before reuse.
- Ritual symbolism does not establish a medical mechanism or guaranteed personal result.

Details, source locations and limits are in the relevant dossiers. No mantra, complete translated scripture, full story, exact ritual yantra or artwork asset is cleared for reuse merely by appearing in this research.
