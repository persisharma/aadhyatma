# Scripture orientation and reading map

An introduction to kinds of religious literature. No single chronology, authorship theory or canon is imposed across traditions.

## Vedic literature

IGNCA's Vedic Heritage Portal presents the four Vedas and the familiar textual divisions. These classifications help readers locate a passage; they are not four interchangeable books with identical contents. [VEDIC-INTRO](sources.md#vedic-intro).

| Topic | Hindi draft | English draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ऋग्वेद / Rigveda | वैदिक साहित्य के चार प्रमुख वेदों में एक। | One of the four Vedas. |
| यजुर्वेद / Yajurveda | यजुर्वेद का परिचय उसकी शाखा और पाठ के साथ दें। | Introduce Yajurveda with its branch and textual context. |
| सामवेद / Samaveda | सामवेद की गायन-परम्परा और संहिता का संदर्भ अलग पहचानें। | Distinguish its chant tradition and textual collection. |
| अथर्ववेद / Atharvaveda | चार वेदों में अथर्ववेद का भी स्वतंत्र स्थान है। | Atharvaveda has its own place among the four Vedas. |
| संहिता / Samhita | मन्त्र-संग्रह का परिचय। | A collection of mantras. |
| ब्राह्मण / Brahmana | वैदिक कर्म और उसके विवेचन से जुड़ा ग्रंथ-वर्ग। | A textual class concerned with ritual and its explanation. |
| आरण्यक / Aranyaka | वैदिक परम्परा का चिंतनपरक ग्रंथ-वर्ग। | A reflective class of Vedic literature. |
| उपनिषद् / Upanishad | आत्मा और परम सत्य जैसे प्रश्नों पर विचार के ग्रंथ। | Texts exploring questions such as self and ultimate reality. |

The boundaries and arrangement of these classes vary across Vedic schools. Brahmana as a textual class, Brahman as a philosophical term and Brahma as a deity must not share a definition merely because spellings look similar.

## Shruti, Smriti and counts

**Hindi draft:** श्रुति और स्मृति परम्परागत ग्रंथ-वर्ग हैं। किसी पाठ के परिचय में उसकी अपनी परम्परा और संस्करण बताना अधिक उपयोगी है। “108 उपनिषद्” मुक्तिका की एक प्रसिद्ध सूची का संदर्भ है; यह सभी उपलब्ध उपनिषदों की निर्विवाद कुल संख्या नहीं है।

**English draft:** Shruti and Smriti are traditional textual classifications. Identify the work and its tradition when explaining them. The familiar 108 Upanishads refers to the Muktika list, rather than an uncontested count of every surviving Upanishad.

Reference: [VEDIC-UPANISHADS](sources.md#vedic-upanishads); recensional context in [VEDIC-BRIHAD](sources.md#vedic-brihad). Do not invent exact composition dates or identify traditional attribution with a modern historical proof.

## Other reading families

| Family / example | What the beginner needs | Research basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| रामायण / Ramayana | An epic narrative; distinguish Valmiki's work from later regional retellings and devotional compositions. | [VR-RAMA](sources.md#vr-rama), opening dialogue; later story references in deity dossier. |
| महाभारत / Mahabharata | An epic reading family; exact episode, recension and edition matter. | Orientation only. A dedicated Mahabharata edition and chapter map remains required before episode summaries are added. |
| भगवद्गीता / Bhagavad Gita | Philosophical and devotional dialogue; read a verse with its chapter and commentator. | Two checked translation publications for 4.7, 5.10 and 9.26 in the source ledger. Full eighteen-chapter summaries were not collated here. |
| पुराण / Purana | Religious narrative, genealogy and cosmology in varied works. | [VP-INTRO](sources.md#vp-intro). The five traditional themes include creation, re-creation, lineages, manvantaras and dynastic histories. Not every work follows a simple five-chapter template. |
| आगम / Agama | Textual traditions addressing worship and related subjects; identify the relevant sectarian corpus. | [KAMIKA](sources.md#kamika), a Shaiva example; not proof that all Agamas share identical ritual. |
| देवी-माहात्म्य / Devi Mahatmya | Locate narrative chapter separately from the recitation adjuncts. | [DM-KALI-PARGITER](sources.md#dm-kali-pargiter), [DM-KALI-AURO](sources.md#dm-kali-auro), [DEVI-KAVACHA](sources.md#devi-kavacha). |
| स्तोत्र / Stotra | A hymn of praise; its traditional attribution and known publication history may differ. | [NAVAGRAHA](sources.md#navagraha), [ASHTALAKSHMI-STOTRA](sources.md#ashtalakshmi-stotra). The two hymns do not establish a universal author/date for all stotras. |
| भाष्य / Bhashya | Commentary explaining a base text. | [BU-MADH](sources.md#bu-madh) explicitly presents Shankara's commentary with the Upanishad translation. It must not be silently quoted as the base verse. |

### What a source label should contain

Work → section/chapter/verse → recension where relevant → editor/translator → publisher → date consulted → whether the sentence is text, translation, commentary or a new Vedansh summary. A URL by itself does not identify those layers.

## Beginner reading paths

These are Vedansh editorial reading suggestions within this pack, not religious prescriptions.

1. **Names and groups:** foundations → thirty-three-devata dialogue → source variants → Dikpal.
2. **What I see in worship:** twelve symbols → plant/material identity → temple image and darshan.
3. **Forms and narratives:** deity identity seed → selected avatara or goddess collection → complete independently collated story.
4. **How to read a reference:** Vedic classes → scripture/translation/commentary distinction → Gargi and Maitreyi dialogue examples.
5. **Time vocabulary:** five Panchang limbs → lunar months → local time label → existing live Panchang.

## Text handling and rights

This research provides original summaries, not a cleared corpus of Sanskrit or translated scripture. Taylor's ANU Vishnu Purana is published under CC BY-NC-ND; consult it as scholarship and do not copy or adapt that translation for a commercial app without suitable permission. BBT, Drik and modern translations also have their own rights.

Before any quoted recitation: use a complete selected passage, collate two independent authoritative publications under RULEBOOK §11, identify edition/translator, and verify rights. Two web mirrors of the same edition do not meet source independence.
