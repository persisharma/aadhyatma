# Editorial decisions, exclusions and remaining checks

This document distinguishes completion of a research pass from permission to publish religious text.

## What this pack provides

Source-linked dossiers for every collection in the approved feature scope; original Hindi/English introductory copy; complete selected rosters where text was located; shorter profile seeds for broader coverage; source-quality notes; a machine-readable topic index; and an app-content reuse audit.

It does not claim that every indexed topic has a full article, two independently collated editions or a completed story. The index's evidence status records that distinction.

## Evidence method

1. Search for the exact passage or institutional publication.
2. Open the relevant page/excerpt; do not treat a search snippet as a read source.
3. Identify work, edition, publisher and the part actually consulted.
4. Keep scripture, translation, commentary, regional practice and new editorial explanation separate.
5. Compare accounts when a list or interpretation changes.
6. Record failed access and identity/OCR problems.
7. Draft original short summaries, preserving uncertainty.

The ledger records page/excerpt access, not “entire book read.” Search-engine crawl dates and a page's cached today label are not the research date or the date of an event.

## Source hierarchy and independence

- A located Sanskrit passage plus identifiable scholarly/traditional editions is strongest for a textual assertion.
- A temple's own publication is primary evidence for that temple's practice.
- Museums establish specific object identity, provenance and described iconography; they do not prescribe every ritual.
- IMD and NASA supply astronomical definitions, not devotional authority.
- Practitioner education supplies attributed interpretations.
- Dictionaries establish possible word senses, not every compound's intended meaning.
- Mirrors, common translations and multiple pages from one publisher do not independently confirm one another.

No unit is marked publication-cleared. RULEBOOK §11's two independent authoritative publication requirement still applies to shipped religious text, including exact quotations. The bibliography's record count must never be advertised as that many independent authoritative editions.

## Claims excluded from copy

No bell-kills-bacteria claim, healing frequency, pineal-gland tilaka mechanism, guaranteed wealth, birth prediction, fertility guarantee, cure, curse-based directional advice or contemporary social stereotyping is adopted. Do not convert religious praise of a result into a measured promise.

Do not present a Purana cosmography as a modern map, invoke NASA as proof of astrology, prescribe extreme fasting from a story, or infer plant/food safety from sacred status.

## Rights and reuse

Use the original summaries as drafts, not a substitute for review. No inferred permission exists to reproduce modern translations, stotra recordings, site drawings or paintings. A museum's Public Domain label applies to the identified asset, not every image from that domain. Check actual asset records before downloading or integrating.

## Engineering handover

Research files live under docs only. App code, images, routes, content bundles, Panchang calculations and releases are untouched. Later integration must follow the Vedansh build checklist afresh.

Documentation checks here validate syntax, internal references, coverage and roster arithmetic; they do not validate religious truth. Native UT/E2E/UI verification is not completed by this research and remains pending for implementation. The PR stays draft.
