# Temples, darshan and practice

Checked institutional teaching and practitioner accounts support a beginner's vocabulary. Architecture and worship vary; one building's plan or visit sequence is not mandatory everywhere.

## Architectural vocabulary

The government Publications Division's Temple Architecture In India article explains the basic parts. Its wording closely parallels NCERT, so the two should not be counted as independently authored corroboration. [TEMPLE-GOV](sources.md#temple-gov).

| Hindi / term | Short definition |
| --- | --- |
| गर्भगृह / Garbhagriha | मुख्य प्रतिमा का भीतरी कक्ष। The inner sanctum housing the main image. |
| मण्डप / Mandapa | मंदिर का स्तम्भयुक्त सभा या प्रवेश-स्थान। A pillared hall or porch. |
| शिखर / Shikhara | उत्तर भारतीय मंदिरों के संदर्भ में ऊपरी अधिरचना का परिचित नाम। A familiar term for the superstructure in North Indian temple discussions. |
| विमान / Vimana | दक्षिण भारतीय संदर्भ में गर्भगृह के ऊपर की अधिरचना का शब्द। A term for the sanctum's superstructure in South Indian discussion. |
| गोपुर / Gopura | प्रवेश-द्वार का ऊँचा भाग। A monumental gateway tower, distinct from the sanctum superstructure. |
| ध्वजस्तम्भ / Dhvajastambha | मंदिर का ध्वज-स्तम्भ। A temple flagstaff. |
| वाहन / Vahana | मुख्य देवता से जुड़ा वाहन-रूप। The deity's associated mount. |
| प्राकार / Prakara | मंदिर-परिसर का घेरा या परिक्रमा-मार्ग, संदर्भानुसार। An enclosure or surrounding passage, according to context. |

Gopura and prakara are supported by [HA-TEMPLE](sources.md#ha-temple); the remaining basic structural terms by the government article. The table is vocabulary, not a complete architectural typology.

**Nagara and Dravida:** useful broad architectural categories with regional subtypes. Do not label every actual temple solely by north/south geography. A generic illustration needs an explicit architectural example and should not combine a Nagara sanctum tower with an unrelated gateway and call it universal.

## Image and darshan

The Met teaching resource describes viewing a sacred image as part of darshan and discusses consecrated images, offerings and temple circumambulation. It also distinguishes a worship setting from viewing an object in a museum. [MET-EDUCATORS](sources.md#met-educators), printed pp 33–34.

**Hindi draft:** मंदिर में प्रतिमा केवल देखने की कलावस्तु नहीं होती; भक्त उसे उपासना के संदर्भ में देखते हैं। दर्शन, पूजा और मंदिर की अपनी व्यवस्था को साथ समझें। किसी संग्रहालय की प्रतिमा का ऐतिहासिक विवरण और किसी जीवित मंदिर की आचार-व्यवस्था अलग जानकारी हैं।

**English draft:** In a temple, an image belongs to a living setting of worship. Understand darshan and puja alongside that temple's own practices. An art-historical object description and a functioning temple's rules answer different questions.

Do not equate darshan only with a ticket or sightline. Detailed theology of divine presence needs a named tradition, not a universal mechanical explanation.

| पूजा / Puja | उपासना और अर्पण का कर्म। Worship involving offerings and attention to the deity. | [HA-TEMPLE](sources.md#ha-temple), temple account. |
| आरती / Arati | देवता के सम्मुख दीप दिखाने से जुड़ा पूजाकर्म। A worship act involving the offered flame. | [SDS-RITUALS](sources.md#sds-rituals). Not identical to every song called an aarti. |
| प्रदक्षिणा / Pradakshina | उपास्य के चारों ओर परिक्रमा। Circumambulation around the sacred focus. | [MET-EDUCATORS](sources.md#met-educators); exact path, count and exceptions are site-specific. |
| अभिषेक / Abhisheka | प्रतिमा पर द्रव्य-अर्पण या स्नान का संदर्भ। Ritual pouring or bathing in a selected worship setting. | [MET-EDUCATORS](sources.md#met-educators). Materials and access require temple-specific guidance. |
| अर्चना / Archana | नामों के उच्चारण और अर्पण से जुड़ी पूजा का एक संदर्भ। A form of worship involving names and offerings. | [HA-TEMPLE](sources.md#ha-temple), personal archana example; not a universal fee or process. |
| नैवेद्य और प्रसाद | अर्पित भोग और प्राप्त प्रसाद की भूमिका अलग समझें। Distinguish what is offered from what is received. | [SDS-PRASADA](sources.md#sds-prasada). |
| उत्सव-मूर्ति / Processional image | उत्सव में बाहर ले जाई जाने वाली प्रतिमा का संदर्भ। An image used in procession. | [MET-SOMASKANDA](sources.md#met-somaskanda) documents carrying rings on one Chola bronze. Exact ritual terminology should be collated for the selected temple. |

## A usable beginner visit introduction

**Hindi:** मंदिर जाने से पहले वहाँ के समय, प्रवेश और आचरण की वर्तमान जानकारी देखें। पूजा के क्रम, जूते रखने, चित्र लेने और गर्भगृह तक जाने की व्यवस्था अलग हो सकती है। प्रार्थना और दर्शन के लिए उपलब्ध स्थान का उपयोग करें और आवश्यक हो तो मंदिर के सेवक से मार्गदर्शन लें।

**English:** Check the temple's current visiting and conduct information. Entry, footwear, photography, worship order and sanctum access can vary. Use the spaces provided for prayer and seek guidance from temple staff where needed.

This paragraph is practical editorial advice. No current temple hours or entry entitlement has been researched. It does not impose one source's gendered prostration rules, clockwise count, donation amount or right-hand procedure on every visitor.

## Diagram and article plan

Create an annotated drawing of **one named architectural type**: gateway → hall → sanctum, with image/mount/flagstaff labels only where present in the reference. Keep temple-type diagrams separate from the eight-direction Dikpal wheel. Historical image labels should include place, period and museum accession number.
