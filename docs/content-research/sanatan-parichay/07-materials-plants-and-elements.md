# Worship materials, sacred plants and elements

This dossier explains identity, use and attributed meaning. It does not create a universal shopping list or a new ritual procedure.

Botanical names should be collated with a botanical authority before a plant-identification guide. Do not transfer a claim from an indigenous/common plant name to a visually similar species. No dosage, ingestion, disease treatment or air-purification performance is inferred from sacred status.

### Sthalavriksha and sacred ecology

The environmental education source distinguishes plants associated with deities, events and particular temples. A **sthalavriksha** is a sacred tree associated with a temple/site; it is not one universal species. **Hindi:** किसी मंदिर का स्थलवृक्ष उस स्थान की अपनी परम्परा से जुड़ा होता है। **English:** A temple's sacred tree belongs to that site's tradition. [CPREEC-PLANTS](sources.md#cpreec-plants).

## Panchamahabhuta

Taittiriya Upanishad 2.1.1 gives a sequence from the Self through space, air, fire, water and earth, continuing to plants, food and the human being. Present it as a traditional account, not a modern periodic table. [TAITTIRIYA](sources.md#taittiriya).

| Hindi | Sanskrit/English reference label |
| --- | --- |
| आकाश | Akasha / space |
| वायु | Vayu / air |
| अग्नि | Agni / fire |
| जल or आप | Ap / water |
| पृथ्वी | Prithivi / earth |

**Hindi:** पंचमहाभूत का परिचय पारम्परिक तत्त्व-विचार से जुड़ा है। आकाश, वायु, अग्नि, जल और पृथ्वी के शब्दों को आधुनिक रसायन-विज्ञान के पाँच पदार्थों की सूची न समझें।

**English:** Panchamahabhuta belongs to traditional reflection on the constituents of experience and world. Its five terms should not be taught as five chemical elements.

Do not attach chakra colours, directions, grahas, organs, personality scores or medical diagnoses to these five simply because they appear together in a modern chart. Each mapping needs an independently located textual basis.

## Cosmology expansion

Vishnu Purana Book Two offers a traditional cosmographic account of regions, mountains and worlds. Treat it as textual cosmology. Do not map its islands or Meru automatically onto contemporary geography or assert a satellite confirmation. [VP2-TAYLOR](sources.md#vp2-taylor).

Possible later units: loka, Meru, Jambudvipa, yuga, manvantara, kalpa. Their detailed measures and mutually different textual systems are **reference backlog**, not researched numerical articles in this pack. Bhagavata 8.13 supplies a manvantara context for the seven sages; that does not by itself verify a complete cosmic chronology.
