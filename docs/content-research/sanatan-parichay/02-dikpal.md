## Direction convention

Use north-up for the educational diagram: east on the right, south below and west on the left. Show the four intermediate directions explicitly. Urdhva and adho are vertical directions, not extra azimuths and not the Vastu mandala centre.

The stable eight-direction association is checked against the Kamika passage and the university course. The ten-direction extension must name its source.

## Object evidence

The Met has a Varuna with a swan and a Sri Lankan Buddhist Varuna with a horse. These are evidence of historically different depictions and religious settings; the Buddhist four-lokapala object is not an interchangeable reference for a Hindu ten-Dikpal illustration. [MET-VARUNA-SWAN](sources.md#met-varuna-swan), [MET-VARUNA-HORSE](sources.md#met-varuna-horse)

## Short explanations

- **Indra:** introduce the eastern guardian and recognizable vajra/elephant attributes. A separate story can later explain a specific Indra narrative.
- **Agni:** introduce the southeastern guardian and fire imagery. Keep ritual fire and scientific combustion explanations distinct.
- **Yama:** introduce the southern guardian without saying south-facing homes or people are cursed.
- **Nirriti:** introduce the southwestern guardian in the chosen textual context; do not turn a name into fear-based home advice.
- **Varuna:** introduce the western guardian and noose; explain that different artworks show different mounts.
- **Vayu:** introduce the northwestern guardian using wind, banner and the selected animal.
- **Kubera:** introduce the northern guardian without promising wealth or a financial result from worship.
- **Ishana:** explain the northeastern Shiva-associated form; keep direction label and deity identity distinct.
- **Brahma:** show the upward extension as source-specific.
- **Below:** present the Vishnu and Ananta accounts as alternatives with references, not as one deity silently substituted for another.

## Hindi draft opening

**चार नहीं दस दिशाएँ कैसे?**

चार मुख्य और चार कोणीय दिशाओं के साथ ऊर्ध्व और अधो को जोड़ने पर दस दिशाओं का परिचय मिलता है। विभिन्न पूजा-परम्पराओं में इनके अधिष्ठाता देवताओं का स्मरण किया जाता है। आठ दिशाओं की परिचित सूची के साथ ऊपर और नीचे के देवताओं का परिचय पढ़ते समय संबंधित ग्रंथ का संदर्भ भी देखें।

## English draft opening

**How do eight compass directions become ten?**

The traditional ten-direction scheme adds above and below to the eight horizontal directions. The guardian identities and their depictions should be read within the selected text or worship tradition.

## Lesson and artwork requirements

Provide a direction list, eight-point wheel and separate above/below controls. A guardian profile contains a source-specific role, name aliases, selected attributes and links to sourced stories. Narratives are optional until a complete arc has been verified.

For artwork, record text/verse, chosen depiction, hands, weapons, mount, composition and deviations. A wheel is an exact diagram, not an image-generated layout. Do not request restricted ritual seed-mantra diagrams as decorative art.
