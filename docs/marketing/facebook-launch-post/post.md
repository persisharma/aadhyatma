## Post copy (English-led)

I built an app. 🙏

Over the last few months I have been working on **Vedansh** — a devotional companion for daily Hindu practice. I wanted one place where the whole family could read, listen and keep their daily sadhana, without ads or sign-ups, and without needing internet.

What is inside:

📖 Bhagavad Gita, Sundarkand, Hanuman Chalisa and many more chalisas, aartis, stotras and kavach — one verse per page, with meaning
📿 Japa mala counter with mantra audio and japa alarms
🗓️ Panchang — tithi, vrat and festival calendar, daily muhurat, reminders
🔮 Kundali, daily rashifal, Guna Milan and Namkaran
🎧 Bhajan library and read-aloud for the Gita and chalisas
🪔 Nitya Sadhana — build your own daily puja routine
🛕 Theerth — Jyotirlingas, Char Dham and more, with their history and yatra guidance
🍚 Puja vidhi, bhog and naivedya for festivals, with kathas
❓ Jijnasa — ask a question, get an answer from the texts
🧭 Vastu compass, home and lock-screen widgets, daily verse and verse sharing

Read in हिंदी · English · ગુજરાતી · ಕನ್ನಡ. Free, works fully offline.

If you or your parents read a chalisa or the Gita every day, I would love for you to try it and tell me what to add next. Please share with anyone who might find it useful. 🙏

👉 Download: https://persisharma.github.io/get-vedansh/
