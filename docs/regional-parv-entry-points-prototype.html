Home vs doorway

Pattern E and this row are both homes — places the feature durably lives. A and B are doorways — places a user who does not yet know the feature exists meets it.

A feature needs one good home and at least one doorway. PRD-42 currently specifies two homes (E, plus the sheet itself) and one dismissible doorway (the seed line). That is the imbalance.

Cost. One row. It is nearly free and duplicates nothing structurally, because MoreScreen rows already deep-link into Panchang-owned surfaces.

Caveat. Two homes for one preference means two places to keep the subtitle truthful. Test-pin both.

Recommendation: keep pattern E as the home; add A + B as the doorways; let Eask carry the two tradition lenses.

A fires at the moment of intent for every geographic lens and reuses a modal that already runs the seeding code. B is the only option that stands permanently on the segment the tab opens on, and it costs nothing on days that have content. C and D are cheap enough to take as well; F is already half-drawn in the signed-off prototype. Onboarding is the one to refuse outright.

None of this touches the engine, the notification families, or any shipped date. It is +1 card, +1 conditional row, +1 ask action, +1 tour step, +1 More row on top of W2-10 — roughly one extra sprint item, all of it OTA-safe.

What changes in the PRD

Open decision 5 is currently answered "pattern E, decided 2026-09-06". It stays decided — E is still the home. What needs adding is a doorway clause: A + B named in §9.1 as W2-10a/W2-10b, the ask action folded into W2-15, and the seed line's "dismissible" behaviour re-scoped, since B replaces the job it was doing badly.

What does not change

The sheet, the lens taxonomy, @vedansh:panchang-lenses, the STATE_LENS holes, the notification gates (W2-12), the lens-blind search (W2-14), and every guardrail. This study moves entry only — exactly as the original placement exploration framed it.
