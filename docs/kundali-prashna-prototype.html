Why it leads instead of joining the grid

The 2×2 tiles answer "what does my chart say about X". प्रश्न answers "what should I do about X". Those are different products, and the second is the one people pay astrologers for.

It also gives जिज्ञासा (the Ask engine, §71) somewhere to route. Typing "क्या मुझे व्यापार शुरू करना चाहिए" into Search today hits no intent. With this shipped, it resolves to prashna.purpose with slot vyapar and opens Screen 3 directly — no new search surface, one new intent in the existing grammar.

Nothing else on the landing moves

The person switcher, the Rashifal card, the four tiles and the closing practice card are untouched. One insert, one fold preserved.

SCREEN 2

Nine purposes, gated by who the chart belongs to

The nine are not a menu — they are bhava sets

Each purpose is a typed record: the bhavas it reads, the natural karakas (significators) it weighs, and which dasha lords and transits count as relevant. Nothing is free-form. See the table in section 4.

The gate is a rule, not a hidden tile

A closed purpose still renders, dimmed, with the reason on it. Hiding it would make the app look broken; showing it with "18 वर्ष के बाद" makes the boundary a visible product decision.

स्वास्थ्य is gated differently for everyone: it reads constitution, routine and the classical 6th/8th significations. It never gives a prognosis, and any symptomatic wording routes to a doctor — this survives the rule change and is non-negotiable.

        Try it

        The evidence chain is the product

        Every claim carries a basis trail — bhava → lord → placement → dasha → gochar — rendered as those small sans chips. Serif is the tradition speaking; sans is the machine showing its arithmetic. A reader who distrusts the sentence can audit the chips.

        That is also what makes the new rule testable: a test walks every emitted indication and fails the build if basis.length === 0. You cannot ship an unsourced sentence even by accident.

        Strength is counted, not asserted

        The pill reads प्रबल / मध्यम / क्षीण from how many independent factors agree. One factor can never render प्रबल. When factors contradict, both columns fill and the सार names the tension instead of picking a side — which is exactly what a careful astrologer does and what a content-farm horoscope never does.

        Timing is a window with real dates

        Pulled from chart.vimshottari antardashas and computeUpcomingIngresses, which already ship. Never "this will happen on" — always a named period with a start and an end the user can check.

        MECHANISM

        How one answer is composed

        Five deterministic passes over data the app already computes. No new ephemeris call, no model, no network — prashna.ts is pure in exactly the way gochar.ts and eventMuhurat.ts are, and the same source-purity test guards it.

        01

        Gather — the purpose's bhavas and karakas

        From chart.houses and chart.grahas: for each bhava in the purpose, its sign, its lord, where that lord sits, and who occupies it. Plus the natural karakas' own placements and dignity. Roughly 12–20 typed facts per purpose.

        02

        Weigh — each fact becomes a signed factor

        A lord in a kendra or trikona supports. A lord in 6/8/12 resists. Exalted supports; debilitated resists; own sign supports; retrograde qualifies. Karaka in its own bhava-karakatva supports strongly. Each factor carries its basis trail from the moment it is created — there is no later step that "adds sourcing".

        03

        Time — overlay dasha and gochar

        Does the running Mahadasha or Antardasha lord own or occupy one of the purpose's bhavas? Is a slow graha transiting them right now (computeGocharSnapshot)? When does the next antardasha that touches them begin (chart.vimshottari)? This pass produces the काल windows with real dates.

        04

        Resolve — strength, and the tension

        Net the supporting and resisting factors. Three or more independent supports and no strong resistance → प्रबल. Mixed → मध्यम, and the सार must name the contradiction out loud. One factor alone → क्षीण, never more.

        05

        Speak — compose, don't author

        Sentences are assembled from typed phrase tables keyed on (factor kind × strength × age band), the same technique dashaReading.ts already uses. No paragraph is hand-written per chart, so the output stays deterministic, translatable into all four reading languages, and machine-scannable for banned vocabulary.

        What the rule change does and does not open

        Opened: Vedansh may now say what a combination indicates about a person's tendencies, learning, work and direction — because every such sentence carries its derivation and its strength, and both are testable.

        Still closed, and not up for negotiation in this PRD: longevity or death readings; medical, psychiatric, legal or financial directives; predicting another person's misfortune, divorce or death; fear copy or a dosha framed as a curse; gemstone, puja or consultation commerce; luck and compatibility scores; and any Math.random, AI generation, remote feed or network call. The AI/network ban was pinned to the old §14.3 text — it is now re-pinned to its own clause so that liberalising interpretation can never be read as liberalising that boundary.

        The honest risk: a purpose-driven astrologer surface is the point in this app where a user is most likely to make a real decision — quit a job, start a venture, move a child's school — on what it says. The strength pill and the evidence chain exist so that a weak reading looks weak. Ship the chain, or don't ship the purposes.
