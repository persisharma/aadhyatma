शुभ योग — The Additive Half of the Muhurat Engine

PRD-27 (proposed, Q4 round 2) · Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build.
eventMuhurat.ts defines twelve DoshaKeys and zero yogas, and reads the 27 nitya yogas only to extract the two inauspicious ones (yoga.index === 16 → vyatipata, === 26 → vaidhriti). The engine can say what is wrong with a day and has no vocabulary for what is specially right about one. This adds सर्वार्थ सिद्धि, अमृत सिद्धि, रवि and the द्विपुष्कर/त्रिपुष्कर pair as pure tables over primitives PanchangData already carries — nakshatra, vāra, tithi. Annotate-only in v1: a yoga never silently re-ranks a day.
1 · The daily surface — where the asymmetry shows
Panchang is already a daily-open surface (PRD-14's stated goal: "a daily utility people check before acting"). Today its day card can only carry dosha chips. The yoga line sits above them, in the same register — named, bounded by a time window, never scored.

2 · The finder — from permitting to recommending
The Event Muhurat Finder (§60) currently filters days by dosha, so its output is a list of days that are not bad. A yoga annotation gives it a positive signal to show — and the open decision is whether that signal may also re-order the list. v1 says no.

Daily Muhurat "right now".
The highest-traffic placement: the surface users already check before acting. A yoga here is the difference between "nothing is wrong today" and "today is specially suited" — the same facts, a usable answer.
Open questions this prototype does not settle
1. Offset or annotate? Recommended annotate-only for v1 (frame 5 shows why the alternative is expensive). Must be pinned in conventions/shubh-yoga-v1.md either way.
2. Which yogas ship in v1 — सर्वार्थ सिद्धि, अमृत सिद्धि, रवि, द्विपुष्कर, त्रिपुष्कर is the proposed set; guru-puṣya and ravi-puṣya are candidates for the same table.
3. Nakshatra-boundary windows routinely cross midnight, so every yoga window must render through the shipped formatEndInstant — a 12-hour clock plus a short-date suffix when the end falls on a different civil day ("2:12 AM, १५ अक्टू"). The printed-panchang extended-hour style (26:12 for 2:12 the next morning) is not used anywhere in this app and must not be introduced by this feature.
4. Every nakshatra×vāra row is verification-gated like the four existing convention docs. These tables are printed in every published panchang, so this is the cheapest verification of anything content-bearing in either slate.
