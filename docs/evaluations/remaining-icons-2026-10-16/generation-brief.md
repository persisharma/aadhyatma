# Muhurat & Vastu Icon Brief

Fifteen painted icons to replace the last letter tiles in the app: the thirteen Muhurat occasions (गृ, वा, ना …) and the two Vastu doors (दि, घ). Every other list already uses these Storybook miniatures, so the new icons must look like they came from the same set.

## Acceptance checks

Reject and regenerate if any of these fails.

- Background is truly transparent: all four corners have alpha 0, and there is no checkerboard, tile or frame.
- No text, letters, numerals or symbols other than those described.
- Each icon can be told apart from its neighbours at 32 px: #1 doorway, #5 key and #15 house must not read as one another, and #14 must not read as the chakra or sun icons.
- The palette matches the current icons placed side by side: matte burnt ochre, muted brass, warm brown outlines. No shiny yellow, vivid orange, glossy 3D or drop shadows.
- Deliver for each file: the master PNG and the exact prompt used. The app team exports it to the house format (512 × 512 PNG, 256-colour alpha palette, longest visible side 432 px, equal margins, alpha below 8 cleared) and records it in `mobile/assets/icons/storybook/manifest.json`.

## What to send back

For each subject: the master PNG named exactly as listed, and the exact prompt you used. The app team handles the export to 512 × 512 and the manifest entry.
