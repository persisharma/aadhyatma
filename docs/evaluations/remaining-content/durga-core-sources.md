## Edition decisions and variants

### Kavach

Both principal witnesses run through56, ending `लभते परमं रूपं शिवेन सह मोदते`. Verse44 has three lines; verse56 has one. Closing phalashruti is retained, not dropped after directions/body protection. Technical body terms are translated respectfully without censoring intimate anatomy. Traditional health/supernatural claims are framed as the hymn's devotional statements rather than clinical facts.

The selected text keeps first12 Sanskrit units from the app where they agree with these witnesses. Their meanings are newly composed in the new corpus; original legacy meanings remain in the old file. New13–56 follow the full sequence. Standard `आरूढा`, `उद्योतिनी`, `खड्गिनी` are preferred over Drik's short-vowel/`खङ्गिनी` typography, supported by SanskritDocuments. Use `नैर्ऋत्यां` as Drik; SanskritDocuments has the equivalent orthographic `नैरृत्यां`. `सहजा` in50 is shared by Drik/SanskritDocuments, not Rampuri's `सहसा`. `जलजाश्चोपदेशिकाः` in49 follows Drik/Rampuri against SanskritDocuments `चौपदेशिकाः`. `कूष्माण्डा` in51 follows Drik/Rampuri against SanskritDocuments `कुष्माण्डा`. No ritual efficacy claim is added by these spelling choices.

### Argala27 versus25

Use the complete27-stanza order shared by SanskritDocuments and Rampuri: `जय त्वं` first, `जयन्ती` second; separate Dhumranetra5, Raktabija6, Shumbha7; `देवि प्रचण्डदोर्दण्ड...`18; separate householder prayer25 and `तारिणि`26; conclusion27. Drik instead begins with Jayanti, combines/reorders some appeals, has the householder/tarini wording as one stanza24, and closes at25. **Do not splice its25-unit numbering into the27-unit edition.**

Selected7 uses Rampuri's `त्रैलोक्यशुभदे`, the standard form for the three worlds; SanskritDocuments prints `त्रिलोक्यशुभदे`. Both refer to the same invocation. The refrain is fully present in every3–26 unit, not elided with “repeat.” V25 is honestly explained as a traditional male householder's prayer for a wife, with present-day mutual companionship context clearly explanatory. The optional two alternative dhyanas and viniyoga are outside this numbered hymn.

### Kilak14 versus16

Use Drik/Rampuri14-stanza text through `ऐश्वर्यं यत्प्रसादेन...`. SanskritDocuments differs in2–6,9–11,14 and adds15 `चण्डिकां हृदयेनापि...` and16 `अग्रतोऽमुं महादेवकृतं...`. Those are not silently appended to a different recension. The chosen14-stanza publication explicitly closes with `कीलकस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम्`. Drik's orthographic `इत्थंरुपेण`8 becomes `इत्थंरूपेण`, as Rampuri; `सोमार्ध`1 retained rather than doubled `सोमार्द्ध`. V3 `देवी` follows Drik (Rampuri `देवि`); meaning treats the hymn as praise of the goddess, not a forced grammatical emendation. Ritual terms such as uccatana are explained, not operationalised into instructions to harm anyone. V13 `उच्चकैः` is explained with the abundance of सम्पत्ति, not as an invented requirement to chant loudly.

### Chapter1 numbered completeness

104 traditional units =14 speaker announcements +24 half-verses +66 full verses. Exact speaker-unit numbers:1,20,26,29,35,39,46,59,63,72,88,96,99,102. The104-unit count is not “104 full metrical shlokas.” No concatenation across these boundaries changes canonical numbering. The story covers Suratha, Samadhi, Medhas' teaching on attachment/Mahamaya, Brahma's hymn, Vishnu's awakening and Madhu-Kaitabha's defeat.

Primary Drik readings are retained in12 `ममत्वाकृष्टचेतनः` (Rampuri `मानसः`),69 `एकाग्रहृदयस्थितः` (Rampuri separates `एकाग्रहृदयः स्थितः`),74 `यानुच्चार्या विशेषतः` (Rampuri `यानुच्चार्याविशेषतः`),78 `महासुरी` (Rampuri `महेश्वरी`),83 `तदा` (Rampuri `मया`),98 `मम` (Rampuri `मया`). Explanations match the selected readings. Orthographic word-boundary spacing is normalised without adding/removing words.

Two source-supported corrections:73 Drik `त्वं स्वधां` becomes `त्वं स्वधा`, corroborated by Rampuri and the predicate construction;104 Drik `श्रृणु` becomes `शृणु`, as Rampuri. V103 `च्छिन्ने` follows Drik's sandhi; Rampuri `छिन्ने` is equivalent separation. Rampuri's parenthetical extra line in101 (`प्रीतौ स्वस्तव युद्धेन...`) is absent in Drik and excluded from this selected104-unit main reading. Optional opening `ॐ ऐं`, closing `ऐं ॐ`, viniyoga/dhyana and colophon are not standalone chapter units. The included text begins with Markandeya's speaker announcement and ends with the sage's invitation to hear further of Devi.

## Legacy Kavach preservation and integration contract

The old `durga-kavach.json` was restored **byte-for-byte from HEAD**. The complete text is a new `durga-devi-kavacham` corpus with IDs `durga-devi-kavacham-1`…`-56`. No old ID is assigned new wording. Parent integration retains old18-unit payload only for existing saved references, labels it a legacy selection and hides its catalogue entry from browse/search/recommendations. Only the complete56 is offered for new reading. This avoids a false migration of mixed half-verses and unrelated prayers.

| Old ID / zero-based index | Relationship to selected full text | Safe handling |
|---|---|---|
| durga-kavach-1…12 /0…11 | Same opening metrical units1…12 | Retain legacy ref; optional explicit ID mapping may point to new1…12 |
| durga-kavach-13 /12 | `रक्षन्तु मां...वीरे मुण्डे...` not found in the selected full Kavach witnesses | Preserve legacy; no canonical equivalence asserted |
| durga-kavach-14 /13 | `शूलेन पाहि नो देवि...` is a separate prayer, not a unit of the selected Kavach | Preserve legacy; no canonical equivalence asserted |
| durga-kavach-15 /14 | Re-pairs full17 second half +18 first half | Preserve legacy; a single canonical unit would lose part |
| durga-kavach-16 /15 | Full18 second half + a variant of19 first half; old north deity **Kauberi**, selected **Kaumari** | Preserve legacy; not identical |
| durga-kavach-17 /16 | Full19 second half +20 first half | Preserve legacy; do not relabel as one complete stanza |
| durga-kavach-18 /17 | Full20 second half +21 first half | Preserve legacy; do not relabel as one complete stanza |

The old18-unit payload was labelled a recension in its source metadata; comparison does not support that description as a continuous complete Kavach. It is a legacy selection and must not be presented as the full text. Source-report and historical payload retention preserve auditability.
