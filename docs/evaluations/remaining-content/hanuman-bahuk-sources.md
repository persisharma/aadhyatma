# C4: Hanuman Bahuk

Complete44 chhands of the public-domain Tulsidas poem, not a preview or medical recommendation. Reuses the existing flat Stuti reader with per-unit vertical scrolling and44 horizontal units. No new audio. Original concise Hindi/English explanatory prose and hand-curated ASCII Hindi/Awadhi pronunciation; Gujarati/Kannada use the existing re-scripting policy.

## Collation

Read the whole [Drik transcription](https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/hanuman-bahuk/shri-hanuman-bahuk.html?lang=hi) and compared [SanskritDocuments](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_otherlang_hindi/hanumAnabAhuka.html). Both contain shared errors, so comparison was extended to the [Gita Press scan](https://archive.org/details/HanumanBahukGitapress). All65 PDF pages were rendered and inspected, concentrating on original verse lines rather than copying the modern commentary. The scan identifies Tulsidas as poet; its commentary preface is signed Mahavir Prasad Malviya, VS1990. This dates the commentary preface, not necessarily this printing. No claim that the scan uploader grants rights to commentary/art.

The Drik spacing is retained; `bahuk-variants.json` records specific print-supported corrections, including16 ढ़आरो→ढारो,30 त्रिपात→त्रिताप,31 समत्व→समत्थ,34 अँबु चूर→अंबुचर,38 restoration of सरीर, and44 वयो→बयो. Differences that are only orthographic or dialectal are not globally Sanskritized. Chhands1–2 retain6 lines; all others retain4. The scan is clipped around the last line of5, which is supported by both full web transcriptions; no absent line was invented.

The SanskritDocuments page header incorrectly says Vairagya Sandipani, while its body and metadata identify Hanuman Bahuk. That header is not propagated. A second compact Archive scan download returned503 and was not used. Web introductory claims of guaranteed cures are not adopted. The original poem’s first-person suffering and karma language are explained as the poet’s devotional perspective, not a diagnosis or judgment of another person’s illness.

## Review limits

Source collation and schema checks do not replace specialist oral-recitation review. Native rendering, screen-reader pronunciation and font-size/device QA remain pending until executed. No recording has been copied or produced.
