# A6/B2 provenance follow-up — 17 November 2026

Research-only follow-up for consolidated PR #702. Read alongside [A6 checkpoint](../a6-kubera-chalisa/research-checkpoint.md) and [source exclusions](source-exclusions.md). No app payload, registry, artwork or modern translation is added by this investigation.

## Decision

Both exact compositions remain blocked on verified textual provenance or permission. This is an evidence gap, not a conclusion that either composition necessarily has subsisting copyright. Public readability, an anonymous-author claim, a recording credit of “Traditional”, or permission covering a recording/image cannot by itself establish rights to distribute the exact literary text.

B2 has additional complete modern publications now: the earlier exclusion's lack of another readable full witness is no longer the practical obstacle. These are separate publishers, but independent textual lineage is unproven. A verified historical or appropriately licensed full witness remains missing.

## B2: additional witnesses and contradictory primary credits

- [Bhagwanam](https://bhagwanam.com/mangal-ki-sewa-sun-meri-deva-lyrics/), dated 10 August 2024, presents a complete Devanagari performance text from opening through closing and repeated opening. Read directly: lines 30–114. Its Roman section contains repeated and missing segments relative to its Hindi section; do not use that Roman section as authoritative. It links the Narendra Chanchal performance. Article attribution to Anurag Garg is not evidence of authorship of the underlying composition. Footer reserves rights; no reuse grant observed.
- [JaapDaily](https://jaapdaily.com/learn/aarti/mangal-ki-seva/) presents thirteen stanzas and calls authorship unknown. Useful modern comparison, not a dated historical witness or permission. Its meanings are not copied.
- [Eternal Raga](https://eternalraga.com/en/mantras/mangal-ki-seva-kali-aarti) presents thirteen units and says its two sources are Shubh Panchang and Bharat Discovery. That claim is a research lead, not independent verification of those editions. No historical publication evidence or underlying-text licence was established.
- [Official Tips Bhakti Prem publication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1xqYB_oGCo0) explicitly credits Narendra Chanchal, Surinder Kohli and lyricist **Balbir Nirdosh**. The indexed official description includes a complete performance transcription. [Tips Official's 2024 publication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=negm4gPt0es) repeats that credit. Direct YouTube opens failed, but indexed descriptions were readable and checked; these are metadata observations, not an audiovisual transcription or verified original edition.
- [T-Series-distributed topic recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fUCbT3FqkXs), supplied by Super Cassettes Industries, identifies Lakhbir Singh Lakkha, Durga-Natraj and **Traditional**, with release date **1999-07-29**. Its phonogram notice is not literary permission. This is an earlier dated recording lead, not a historical full printed witness. The indexed description was readable; direct open failed.
- [Bhakti Veena](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tbx4bwU3u_4) also credits traditional lyrics while reserving audio/video rights. This does not resolve Tips' named-lyricist credit.

Interpretation: the conflicting metadata may reflect an adaptation, arrangement, or inaccurate credit. Do not decide which without supporting evidence; do not assert that Balbir Nirdosh necessarily authored every circulated word. Full modern witnesses can support collation once a rights-supported base is identified, but cannot settle provenance merely by agreeing.

Textual cautions: printed thirteen-stanza grouping and performance grouping differ; performances insert additional refrains. The Saturday attendant term has spelling/interpretation variants. No meaning is invented to conceal that uncertainty. Bhagwanam's Hindi and Roman versions are not interchangeable.

The Bharat Discovery candidate URL `https://bharatdiscovery.org/india/काली_माता_की_आरती` returned **403** on this attempt. Therefore its full text and page-specific licensing were not verified. The previously recorded 1930 Marwadi anthology OCR search remains inconclusive; no claim of absence from the actual printed volume is made.

## Search coverage and reproducibility

Queries included exact composition/title searches with `लेखक`, `पुस्तक`, `पुरानी`, `1930`, `license`, `copyright`, `public domain`, `गीता प्रेस`, `site:hi.wikisource.org`; Kubera searches also used `मानस`, `कृष्णदत्त`, `Krishnadatt`, `Manas author`, and `Devesh Gupta`; Kali searches used `Balbir Nirdosh Tips`, `Traditional`, `1993`, and `1999-07-29`.

No exact historical full publication or compatible explicit literary reuse grant was verified from those results. This is a bounded search result, not an exhaustive bibliographic claim. Old checkpoint archive/catalogue results were read as prior evidence, not represented as newly rerun searches. Search-engine snippets and current website dates do not date the original composition.

## What would unblock shipping

One verifiable historical full edition whose publication/authorship evidence supports reuse of this exact text, or permission/licence covering the exact composition from a party entitled to grant it. Then collate against at least one other complete witness, document unit order and variants, and write fresh Hindi/English meanings and checked Roman pronunciation. A competitor APK or rewritten meanings cannot replace this evidence. No external permission request was sent, no purchase was made, and no PR was created by this worker.
