Each full stanza has Hindi text, pronunciation-based Latin text and original Hindi/English meaning. A two-way comparison of selected verses and primary-publication lines found no omitted stanza or unmatched line except three explicitly recorded spelling normalizations: सिंहों/सिहों and करुणा/करूणा in Ambe, and नीलाम्बर/निलाम्बर in Shani. Both publications were read; spelling, spacing and refrain layout were compared, and substantive variants are retained in each JSON source record. The source sites are text-verification references, not permissions for their images, audio recordings or translations. Textual signatures such as Prempal, Dhyanu and Gurdita remain in the hymns; this work makes no invented biographical attribution.

## App placement and value

- Aarti category and deity lists: the missing worship compositions appear beside the existing aartis. The shorter Ganga/Shani entries and the complete longer Ekadashi naming cycle serve different practices without a new navigation category.
- Search and purpose discovery use the same canonical IDs. Santoshi/Ahoi/Shakambhari gain family-devotion associations; these are browse metadata, not promises of medical, financial or fertility outcomes.
- Existing bookmarks retain their positions: Jay Adhya Shakti stays at 10, Ganga follows at 11, and the nine new entries append at 12–20.
- Saraswati Moola Mantra uses manual japa, source provenance and the existing audio-unavailable state. It is a true text gap; Mahamrityunjaya, Ganesh Mool, Shani Pranama and Surya Pranama were already present elsewhere and are not duplicated.
- Dedicated Shani/Kubera/Ganga backgrounds are reused. Durga forms use existing Durga art; Ahoi/Tulsi/Ekadashi use neutral diya art. Vishnu tags on Tulsi/Ekadashi describe catalog association.
