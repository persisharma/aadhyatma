What changed in the last commit

The द्वार was drifting toward a fundraising portal. Three structural corrections, each pinned by a test rather than by editorial restraint.

The mahatva moved to the प्रयोजन

Each of the nine causes now carries mahatvaHi/En — why this kind of giving is held dear — rendered above the places that serve it, grouped and filtered alike.

Five carry a paired citation and a review-only source. Four — वृद्ध, आरोग्य, जीव, आपदा — carry none, because none was found they could honestly carry. Cite and source travel as three-or-none.

A row became a place, not a profile

Dropped registrationHi/En; collapsed officialUrl and donateUrl into one link on the org's own domain.

DaanOrgEntry can no longer hold account numbers, UPI ids, registration/80G text or scale figures — a test greps types.ts for those field names, so re-adding one fails the suite rather than shipping.

Verification went behind the curtain

The §6.2 bar is unchanged; it stopped being rendered. The old detail screen showed registration kinds and a “✓ verified against two independent sources” line.

That reads as the app vouching for an organization it cannot audit, and it made paperwork the last thing read before leaving. verifiedOn stays in the data, driving isOrgRowStale, and is never shown.

The nine प्रयोजन and what anchors each

The cause axis is not the ledger axis. DaanCategory records the dravya — what was given. DaanCause names whom it serves, and is the द्वार's only grouping.

प्रयोजनanchor for the mahatvacitationpurpose bridgerows

wealth and prosperity stay unbridged. The app's text-intent taxonomy has both, and a give-door on a prosperity intent is exactly the fruit-promise the stance guard bans. The bridge that does exist opens the educate home, never the द्वार — a source-shape test pins it.
