Vedansh (Sanskrit: "a portion of the Vedas") is a beautifully designed bilingual companion for Hindu devotion — sacred texts, a daily Panchang, and a practice you keep. Immerse yourself in timeless scriptures with a reverent, book-like reading experience, fully offline.

DAILY BHAKTI
- A fresh devotional verse every day with meaning, on a beautiful painted card
- Read it in your chosen language and share it as an image

SACRED LIBRARY
- Bhagavad Gita: all 18 chapters, 701 shlokas with meaning and commentary
- Sundarkand, Ramcharitmanas selections and Vishnu Sahasranama
- Chalisas, aartis, stotrams and sanskar for children
- Illustrated children's stories and an in-app Bālakāṇḍa Ramayana comic
- Instant search across the whole library

BILINGUAL READING
- Every verse in Hindi (Devanagari) and English — now also Gujarati and Kannada
- IAST transliteration for Sanskrit texts
- One verse per page with swipe navigation, or hands-free auto-slide
- Adjustable reading size and a beautiful parchment-style design

DAILY PANCHANG
- Accurate tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and sunrise/sunset for your city, from a real astronomy engine
- Purnimant and Amanta systems, Vikram Samvat and a full month calendar
- Daily Muhurat: Choghadiya, Rahu Kaal and Abhijit at a glance
- Location is optional — a city picker (default Ujjain) works without it

VRAT, FESTIVALS & KATHAS
- Browse vrats, festivals and 80+ kathas: Ekadashi, Satyanarayan, Karwa Chauth, Shivaratri and more
- Illustrated, step-by-step Puja Vidhi with samagri and bhog guidance
- Follow the ones you observe and get gentle reminders

DAILY SADHANA
- Build a daily or weekday routine with the right deity for each day
- Take a Sankalp: guided 4-to-41-day programs, all the way to completion
- Track your reading and celebrate completion

JAPAM MEDITATION
- Digital bead counter for 108-bead mala rounds
- Turn any stotram into a japa — one bead for every complete recitation
- Japam alarms: wake to your chosen mantra, with repeat days and skip-a-day
- Multiple mantras: Om Namah Shivaya, Mahamrityunjaya, Gayatri Mantra, Hare Krishna and more

LISTEN
- A built-in player for bhajans, mantras and aartis
- Background audio for uninterrupted listening

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
- The daily verse, today's Panchang and your Japam count at a glance, without opening the app

JYOTISH & VASTU
- Guna Milan: Ashtakoota 36-guna kundali matching for marriage compatibility
- Vastu Disha: a live direction chakra with the classical directions for the mandir, kitchen, main door, sleeping and tulsi

THEERTH - SACRED PILGRIMAGE
- Explore India's dhams on a real-geography map, by state, category or yatra
- Sourced temple stories for each site

YOUR SPIRITUAL JOURNEY
- Sadhak Profile: track reads, beads, rounds and streaks
- Bookmark and revisit favorite verses
- Resume reading from where you left off
- Shareable verse cards

BROWSE BY DEITY
- Explore texts by Rama, Krishna, Shiva, Hanuman, Durga or Ganesha

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account, no ads, no tracking. Works fully offline; your practice stays on your device.

All texts are from public-domain Hindu classics, presented with reverence for personal study and devotion.
