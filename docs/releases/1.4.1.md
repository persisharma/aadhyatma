# Vedansh 1.4.1 — Release Notes

- **iOS build:** 30 (EAS production profile may auto-increment at build time)

## App Store "What's New" copy

> 🪔 A big update for your daily devotion.
>
> • **Panchang** — A daily Hindu almanac powered by a real astronomy engine: accurate tithi,
>   nakshatra, yoga, karana and sunrise/sunset for your exact location.
> • **Vrat & Festivals** — Browse vrat and festival kathas (Ekadashi, Satyanarayan, Ganesh,
>   Shivaratri and many more), follow the ones you observe, and get reminders.
> • **Daily Routine (नित्य साधना)** — Build a weekday practice with a deity of the day, track
>   your reading, and celebrate completion.
> • **Theerth** — Explore India's sacred pilgrimage sites on a real-geography map, by state,
>   category, or yatra.
> • **New languages** — Read in Gujarati and Kannada, alongside Hindi, English and Sanskrit.
> • **Search** — Instantly find any text, chalisa, aarti or mantra across the whole library.
> • **Daily reminders** and **shareable verse cards**.
>
> Plus a refreshed home screen, more accurate translations and transliterations, new content
> (Saraswati, Bajrang Baan, Krishna Stotram, Sanskar for kids), and many performance and
> stability fixes.
>
> 🙏 Thank you for being part of Vedansh.

## Full changelog since 1.3.1 (internal reference)

### Features
- **Panchang tab** with a real astronomy engine — tithi/nakshatra/yoga/karana, sunrise/sunset,
  adhik-month label, slim header + 2×2 anga grid (PRD-07, #67).
- **Location-aware Panchang** — GPS with city-picker fallback (default Ujjain) (#121).
- **Vrat & Festival Kathas** — browse, follow, prioritise and remind; full un-hidden story
  library with in-story narratives (PRD-09, #119).
- **Daily Routine (नित्य साधना)** — weekday deity scheduling, add-to-routine touch-points,
  chapter picker, per-subsection progress; redesigned "Today's Practice" (seal, mala streak,
  offered times) (PRD-10, #87).
- **Theerth** — India pilgrimage map with real geography; browse By State / By Category /
  By Yatra; deity backgrounds and sourced prose (#118).
- **Gujarati & Kannada** reading languages (i18n).
- **On-device global library search** (PRD-03); Search FAB on Home.
- **Daily Bhakti notifications** with multiple reminder times (PRD-01).
- **WhatsApp-shareable verse cards** with app link (PRD-05).

### Content
- New sections: **Saraswati** (#103), **Krishna Stotram**, **Bajrang Baan**, **Sanskar for kids** (#83).
- Sundarkand additions, Mahishasura Mardini restoration, NEW-content badge.

### UI & Polish
- Compact ॐ वेदांश़ ॐ home header, single-line routine chip, **completion celebration**
  (pushpa-varsha) (#110, #113).
- Panchang & Vrat UI polish — legibility, elevation, follow-stars (#126).
- Language-aware bilingual title hierarchy (#95); larger/darker English content (#91).

### Fixes
- Chaptered-reader swipe auto-advance across subsection boundaries.
- Default-enabled daily reminders never firing on fresh install (#75).
- Translation/placeholder fixes in chalisas & aartis (#98); ITRANS→IAST romanization
  (RULEBOOK 10.12); content-correctness audit across texts.
- Cold-start data-loss fixes; stable verse-of-the-day (#100).

### Performance
- Panchang: lazy-load day data, precompute observance dates, bound festival lookups, defer
  day observances off the render path (fixes tab freeze).

### Infra
- CI + library-integrity test + RULEBOOK enforcement hooks; llm-wiki bootstrap;
  Android Play Store config (Android not part of this iOS release).
