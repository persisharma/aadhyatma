The plan behind the pixels

1. What is reused verbatim, and the one place the vrat model does not fit

PRD-16 §6.7 says "Reuse the vrat follow-and-remind pattern verbatim." The pattern transfers exactly — a local-first follow context, a pure planner with now parameterised, thin scheduling glue, a headless scheduler component, one identifier prefix. The store does not, because of one structural difference:

Vrat follow (shipped)Muhurat follow (new)
KeyruleId — a recurring ObservanceRule{occasionId}:{YYYY-MM-DD} — one specific day
Next datecomputed each time by the festival enginethe date is the key
Lifetimeforever — Ekadashi comes round againone-shot; prunes once past
Priority under the capfollow order (user-ranked)soonest first — a dated one-shot has no other sane ranking
Time granularitythe day (07:00 morning notice)the window (6:07 AM) — hence the clamp
Empty state—the same follow, targeting the first qualifying date — one kind, not two (see screen 5)
Pref modelSHARED — VratReminderPref (advanceDays 0|1|2|3, dayOf, dayOfTime) is imported, not re-declared. Default stays 1 day + 07:00.

3. Gotchas this slice has to get right

  • Never store a computed time. A follow stores occasionId + civil date only. The window is re-derived from panchangDayStore at plan time, because every window is sunrise-derived and a city change moves all of them (design.md §60, PRD §6.1). Storing "6:07 AM" would silently lie the moment the user travels.
  • Re-plan on location and calendar-system change — the same two inputs that scope the day store. This is the scheduler's trigger set, not just "follows changed".
  • Verdict drift. A followed day can re-grade to excluded after a location change. Keep the follow, mark the row in words, suppress its notices — never fire a reminder for a day the engine now rejects (screen 6, row 3).
  • Prune or the store lies. One-shot follows must be dropped once past, or the ★ count grows forever and reminderCount counts notices that can never fire.
  • The shared iOS 64-slot budget now has seven claimants. Cap at 8 and rank soonest-first; log the truncation count the way planVratReminders does.
  • Day-of clamp. min(chosen dayOfTime, windowStart − 30 min). Without it, an early-Amrit window (6:07 AM here) gets a 07:00 notice that arrives after the muhurat opened — the feature failing at the one job it has.
  • FOR TODAY ordering is test-enforced. festiveReminders.test.ts asserts the leading card on the 18 catalogued festivals; the abujh card goes after the festival tier or that suite goes red.
  • No quiet hours (removed in #70). No new palette — saffronTint/goldChip/avoidChip only, word + tint never colour alone (§12). All copy authored Devanagari + English through contentByLang; the followed-state times use tabular-nums + nowrap so font-scale L cannot wrap them (PRD §6.1).

4. Test & e2e gates (RULEBOOK §0, §9)

  • muhuratReminderPure.test.ts — advance/day-of fire times, the clamp, cap truncation ordering, season-open kind, and the prune boundary at midnight. Runs under npm run test:data beside vratReminderPure.test.ts.
  • MuhuratFollowContext.test.tsx — normalize/de-dup/prune, mirroring the shipped VratFollowContext.test.tsx.
  • A routing test that a location change re-plans (the same shape as the day-store routing test that caught the RETAINED_PAST_DAYS bug by counting engine calls, not by inspection).
  • .maestro/muhurat-follow-smoke.yaml — finder → occasion → day detail → follow → sheet → save → followed state → MyVrat shows the row; plus the empty-with-reason season-follow path.
  • design.md §60 and RULEBOOK §17 updated in the same PR (design-doc-sync rule) — §60's "Known Phase-1 limits" line currently reads "follow/remind is the next slice" and must move to a described surface.

5. Open questions worth deciding before code

  • Should the empty state also NAME the season boundary? Resolved for the follow itself (it targets the first qualifying date), but the copy could still say "चातुर्मास 21 नवम्बर को समाप्त" as information. That needs a festival-engine lookup and inherits the kshaya ambiguity (Dev Uthani 2026 is listed as both 20 and 21 Nov, PRD §9.2) — deferred.
  • Follow the day, or follow the occasion? Following वाहन क्रय as a standing interest ("tell me when a श्रेष्ठ day appears") is a larger, more valuable feature — and a much bigger one, since it needs a background re-scan. This slice deliberately follows days. Worth naming as a Phase-2 candidate rather than leaving implied.
  • Does the Today-strip chip window stay at 7 days? Shorter keeps Home quiet; longer makes the follow feel alive.
  • Should an abujh day be followable too? Screen 7 only announces it. An abujh day has no window to clamp to, so it would reuse the plain vrat 07:00 shape — cheap, but it adds a third follow kind.
