कथा साझा करें · one card, or a carousel

Interactive prototype for PRD-45: a share button on every content surface, and multi-page (carousel) export when one card cannot hold the text. Every control in the phone works; the side panel explains the decision behind it.

Instagram does not accept a multi-image share intent. strong inference No app can hand Instagram a ready-made carousel — the share extension takes one image into Feed or Story. The only working carousel path is Save all pages to Photos → open Instagram → New post → Select multiple. The design below is built around that, not around a share sheet that would silently drop pages 2–N.

Second constraint: saving to Photos needs expo-media-library, which is not in the current build — so the carousel row is a store build, while universal single-card share and the pagination engine ship over OTA first.

    How the text is cut into pages

    Same idea as the existing fitMeaningType ladder: geometry is known (540×675 dp card, 28/28/22 padding, header, ornament, footer), so the line budget is arithmetic, not a layout pass. The estimator below runs live on the text in the phone.

    Rules. Greedy sentence packing (split at । ॥ . ? !), paragraph gap costs half a line, page 1 also carries the title. A paragraph that overflows is continued on the next page with a leading …. A trailing page under 3 lines is re-balanced with the page before it. Hard cap MAX_PAGES = 10: past it the sheet refuses “full” scope and asks for a section — a 20-slide carousel is legal on Instagram but nobody reads one. strong inference

    What this is not. Not a measured layout: like the meaning ladder it uses a chars-per-line estimate (hi 44 · en 54 at the card's body sizes) and will be verified by shareCardFit-style tests that render each page and assert no clipped Text. If a real device shows overflow, lower the constant — never add adjustsFontSizeToFit (design.md §39 records why).
