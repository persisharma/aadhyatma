## Provenance and adaptation

Traditional devotional tale, following Swami Sivananda, Essence of Ramayana, “Sri Hanuman”, coronation/pearl-necklace episode; compared with Abstracts from Ramayana, Sadguru Sri Nannagaru, printed pp. 160–161.

https://www.dlshq.org/religions/sri-hanuman/
https://www.srinannagaru.com/books/RamayanaBySadguruNannagaru.pdf

Original four-language child retelling of a popular devotional tradition, not a quotation or a claimed passage of Valmiki Ramayana. Sivananda supplies the selected sequence: coronation, Sita’s gift, pearl inspection, Sita’s questions, Rama and Sita within Hanuman’s heart, Rama’s embrace. Nannagaru independently corroborates the gift, inspection and heart reveal; his question is worded differently. Do not substitute Vibhishana or an unnamed challenger for Sita. The physical chest-opening image is represented gently as divine radiance with no wound or blood. No invented dialogue, hostility, restoration scene or added boon. Takeaway is editorial.

User reference: IMG_9235.jpeg, an Instagram screenshot of the heart reveal. It identifies the requested tale; the screenshot is not reproduced as app artwork or used as a textual source. No screenshot text, influencer branding or generated social-poster art is copied.

The complete outline below was established before commissioning scenes. A single scene carries each principal beat. Sita remains the questioner throughout the selected account.
