# Kids story authoring and pacing checklist

Required by RULEBOOK §29 for every new story and every narrative revision. Copy the review template below into the story's checked-in provenance directory. Complete the outline before captions/art; revisit the entire sequence after any insertion or rewrite. Tests cannot detect whether a child understands a causal transition.

## Before writing or illustrating

- [ ] Identify the base text, edition, dated publication references and adaptation boundaries; compare two publication sources under §11. Keep regional/traditional variants distinct.
- [ ] Outline the whole arc: setting and characters → motive → action → response → consequences → resolution. These are review questions, not a prescribed six-page structure.
- [ ] Explain each character's identity/role before that character changes the story.
- [ ] For every adjacent pair of pages, answer: why does this happen next, who acts, what changed, and what response leads to the outcome?
- [ ] Resolve missing causes/reactions with a sourced scene. Keep one principal beat per caption; use another scene when a caption contains several major events. Do not add invented dialogue or filler to meet a page count.
- [ ] Preserve existing stable page IDs during revisions; add IDs for inserted beats and update the expected sequence/counts.

## Before sign-off

- [ ] Read each language from beginning to end without relying on source notes to explain missing events. Compare the event sequence across all four languages.
- [ ] Review the exact illustration against each page narrative, including intermediate reactions and the ending; a matching story title/cover is insufficient.
- [ ] Extend the relevant Maestro flow to visit every page, verify its stable ID/counter and full non-final caption, scroll the ending/source and return to the shelf. Confirm locale changes preserve the page.
- [ ] Inspect the actual native sequence at Standard and Large reading sizes. Keep captions readable and the complete illustrated content visible: permit vertical scrolling rather than shrinking art to fit a caption, and trim only a visually reviewed empty bottom band. Do not accept browser-only evidence as native validation.
- [ ] Synchronize browser copies, verify bundled art/translation coverage and byte parity, and run the required repository checks.
- [ ] Retain dated full-sequence evidence, source/art provenance and unresolved release checks. A story with a remaining causal gap is not complete even if tests pass.

## Regression examples: gaps that must not recur

| Story | Compressed transition | Required connection in the sourced arc |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Putana | Nursing immediately becomes death | Establish concealed intention, Krishna's response, Putana's cry/attempt to escape, the disguise falling and then the separate rescue |
| Ganesha Birth | A guardian appears; confrontation jumps to restoration | Explain Parvati's decision, his duty and the escalating conflict, then the plea for life/honor before restoration |
| Hanuman and the Sun | Flight jumps to Indra's strike or unexplained revival | Establish Anjana's departure/hunger, Rahu's appeal to Indra, Vayu's response and the world's appeal to Brahma |

Apply these causal questions to every future story, rather than copying these particular scenes into unrelated narratives. Current source references and implemented scene sequences are documented in [the pacing revision report](README.md).
