# Krishna Janma picture book — image prompts

For ChatGPT image generation. Prototype layout: `docs/katha-comic-prototype.html`.

## How to keep characters consistent

1. Generate the **character sheet** (step 0) first. Keep regenerating until you like it.
2. For every page, in the **same chat**, upload the character sheet and say "use these exact characters". Starting a new chat per page makes the faces drift.
3. Ask for **4:5 portrait, 1024×1280**, and repeat "no text, no letters" every time. The model likes to add captions, and the book already has them.
4. Save the files as `kj-01.png` … `kj-10.png`, in page order, and send them to me.

**Style block (paste at the end of every prompt):**

> Odisha Pattachitra painting style, natural mineral pigments on treated cloth, bold black outlines, figures in profile with elongated almond eyes, flat perspective, richly patterned textiles with dot motifs, red and indigo grounds with small floral fillers, ornate double border with a dotted red band, warm aged-cloth texture. Devotional and dignified, suitable for children. No text, no letters, no watermark. 4:5 portrait.

## 0. Character sheet

(2) Devaki — fair skin, red sari with gold border and white dot pattern draped over her head, blue blouse, gold nose ring, red bindi. (3) Kansa — tan skin, tall gold crown with red jewels, thick curled moustache, fierce angled brow, green dhoti with gold border, dark red sash. (4) Baby Krishna — deep blue skin, black curls, peacock feather in his hair, wrapped in yellow cloth. (5) A palace guard — helmet with spike, red tunic, ochre trousers, spear and round shield. [STYLE BLOCK]
