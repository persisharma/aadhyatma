# Full-narrative pacing review — Maa Durga / Navaratri

Review: Codex, 2026-10-09. All four complete authored narratives were read and compared for roles, motives, actions, responses and consequences, including every ending. This is a source/alignment review, not independent Gujarati/Kannada editorial approval. Artwork review is byte-tied in `art-review.json`; native evidence is separate in `validation.md`.

Publication provenance and edition/variant limits: `source-manifest.json`. Original pre-art complete-arc plan: `outline.md`. No unresolved causal gaps were found in the final authored sequence after the corrections below.

- SN2 now identifies the reported beauty as Kaushiki’s and preserves Parvati becoming dark Kalika. SN6 introduces the Goddess’s power before naming Shivaduti.
- BR4 includes Shiva’s eventual consent (2.3.24.66–77), not just the devas’ initial request. SK4 retains the embrace and corrects English syntax.
- SU5 retains three years of worship, not nine festival nights. SA4’s nine nights and SA6–7’s ten-day war/eleventh-day duel are separate intervals.
- Related Parvati stories identify their traditional Navadurga association in source notes; Kushmanda, Kalaratri and Siddhidatri remain introductions.
- Mahagauri uses Shiva Purana 7.1.24–27. Shumbha uses Devi Mahatmya 5–10. Their different Kaushiki origin accounts remain separate.
- Violent outcomes remain causally explicit while images omit injury. Severe fasting, self-blood offerings and self-harm trials are omitted rather than offered as child practices.
