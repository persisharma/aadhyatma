## Source comparison and editorial limits

Consulted on 8 November 2026:

- Hindu American Foundation, *Reporters Guide to the Swastika*: word meaning,
  auspicious household/festival usage, four aims, Vedas, ashramas, yugas,
  directions and sacred-use/history distinction.
- Himalayan Academy, *Loving Ganesha*, chapter 7: Ganesha/sun/life associations,
  four aims, common right-hand form and historical diversity of handedness.
- All World Gayatri Pariwar, *Akhand Jyoti*, Jan–Feb 2005, *Swastika — A symbol
  of Auspicious Knowledge*: opening equal-arm cross and clockwise right-angle
  extension description. Used for geometry only. Its energy-field explanations
  and mistaken claim about Nazi handedness are **not** adopted.
- Existing ISKCON Educational Services source remains in the provenance graph.

Exact URLs are retained in source-provenance.json, never shipped into public UI.
The drawing sequence and everyday examples are app-written educational prose,
not a quoted ritual manual. No fixed aim-to-arm assignment, single mandatory
starting stroke, finger, obligatory dots, inauspicious crossing-line claim or
scientific/wealth guarantee is asserted. Other religious handed forms are not
labelled universally wrong. Materials are customary suggestions, not a mandate.
Independent editorial acceptance remains pending; source-checked-candidate is
not scholarly certification. Gujarati/Kannada follow existing Hindi re-scripting,
not newly authored translations.
