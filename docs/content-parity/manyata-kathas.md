# मान्यता और कथाएँ — Beliefs, their stories, and their teachings

Research manifest of popular Hindu *manyatas* (beliefs/niyams) with the katha behind each, its teaching (seekh), and a source-confidence tag. **This is not shippable content** — per RULEBOOK's verified-only posture, only `[Certain]` rows are candidates, and even those need the primary text pulled and verified before entering `mobile/src/data/`.

**Source tags**
- `[Certain]` — attested in a nameable primary text (Veda/Upanishad, Valmiki, Mahabharata, a Mahapurana, Ramcharitmanas).
- `[Likely]` — Puranic/regional-text roots, but the popular telling is embellished or the attribution is loose.
- `[Guessing]` — folk / oral only; no text known. Treat as lok-manyata.

**Common misconceptions corrected**
- *"Never give water to Tulsi"* — the actual niyam is: don't water or pluck Tulsi on **Sunday and Ekadashi** (and not after sunset). The rule is folk; the Vrinda story behind Tulsi is Puranic.
- *"Shiva gave Lanka to Ravana"* — folk. In Valmiki (Uttara Kanda) Vishwakarma built Lanka for the rakshasas Mali-Sumali-Malyavan; Kubera later lived there; Ravana **seized** it (and the Pushpaka) from Kubera, his half-brother.
