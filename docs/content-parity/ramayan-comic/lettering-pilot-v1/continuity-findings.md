# Continuity findings raised during the lettering pilot

2026-10-12. Read-only review of the two examples raised by the user. These findings invalidate a blanket claim that the existing 137-panel draft has complete narrative/art continuity. This is not a complete audit of the other episodes; no story revisions have been activated by this report.

## 1.28 → 1.29: the question that motivates the flashback is missing

In the draft, `bk-09-02` teaches withdrawal of weapons (1.28), then `bk-09-03` immediately pictures Vamana and Bali (1.29). The phrase “आश्रम की कथा में” does not establish what the travellers have seen or why the sage starts this story. The transition review's “Responsible control of weapons precedes the destination's history” describes adjacency, not an actual narrative connection.

[Bala 1.28.16–22](https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/valmikiramayan/baala/sarga28/balaroman28.htm) supplies the missing scene: as they continue walking, Rama notices the pleasant woodland/hermitage and asks whose hermitage it is and where he must protect the sacrifice. [Bala 1.29.1–4](https://sanskritdocuments.org/sites/valmikiramayan/baala/sarga29/balaroman29.htm) explicitly makes Vishwamitra's history an answer to this question and connects the place to Vishnu/Vamana. These pages were opened in this review. The SanskritDocuments pages mirror the same Valmikiramayan translation and are not a second independent publication. Direct sarga 28 access failed and direct sarga 29 returned an account-suspension page.

Proposed correction, awaiting content review: show the resumed journey and Rama's question; establish Vishwamitra as the narrator of a past event; then enter the Vamana scene; return explicitly to the present-day hermitage. Allocate additional panels rather than compressing the bridge into unrelated artwork. Inspect whether the one-panel Vamana retelling itself adequately conveys the requested land, action and consequence.

**Follow-up, 2026-10-19:** The integrated four-language edition still left Rama's question unanswered before showing Vamana. Page 17 now gives Vishwamitra's answer and introduces the Vamana–Bali account in a caption on the existing approach scene, then returns to the present with the Siddhashrama arrival panel. The same bridge is authored in Hindi, English, Gujarati and Kannada; the art, panel count and row order are unchanged. A text/order regression test covers the transition. The new wording remains an adaptation draft pending human editorial review; independent source collation is still open.

## Tataka: the decisive action and outcome are not visually established

`bk-08-03` shows an ongoing attack. `bk-08-04` says Rama's arrow killed Tataka, but its art brief requests only an aftermath with Tataka obscured. The selected `bk-08-v1.png` goes straight to the sage and princes standing in a calm forest; Tataka's fall is not visible. The reader must infer the entire decisive action from the caption.

[Bala 1.26.22–26](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/baala/sarga26/balaroman26.htm), opened in this review, supplies the sequence: the warning before dusk, continued attack, Rama's decisive arrow, and Tataka's fall/death. Proposed correction: distinct child-appropriate action and consequence beats, such as Rama releasing the arrow, Tataka falling at a distance without gore, then the danger ending. Keep the violence non-graphic while making the event unmistakable. Do not substitute a cheerful forest tableau for the narrated event.

The current aftermath's bright sunlight also needs chronology review: the passage proceeds into sunset, a night halt, and departure the following morning (1.26.31–34). “Sunlight returns” is an artistic metaphor in the old prompt, not a sourced time transition.

## Implication for the remaining comic

Before extending the corrected lettering treatment beyond the selected episode, audit every transition as cause → action → response → consequence, including travel, elapsed time, narrator/flashback entry and return. Compare each illustration with its exact caption. Source coverage and chapter numbering alone do not prove continuity. Corrections need sourced outlines and all-language/editorial review before production acceptance.
