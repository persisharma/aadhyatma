# Whole-book continuity and costume follow-up

Authored 2026-10-22; applied to the illustrated preview 2026-10-25.

All 81 pages and 269 scenes were read in order in Hindi, English, Gujarati and Kannada. This follow-up changes 95 text fields on 52 pages. The earlier review missed real explanatory gaps reported by the user; geometry and complete image coverage did not establish narrative completeness.

## What changed

- Trijata is introduced as an elderly poor Brahmin seeking help for his family. The staff challenge, cows, blessing and move to the household attendants are explained.
- Other entry/response repairs include Sumantra, Manthara, the sages, Guha’s army concern, the messengers’ withheld news, Jabali’s argument, Bharata’s motives and the sandals/regency decision.
- Dialogue now uses relationship-specific respectful forms, including Lakshmana’s Ram Bhaiya / Sita Bhabhi watch line, with corresponding forms in all four languages. Elders naming younger family members remains natural.
- Ten page paintings were replaced after visual review: 34, 52, 57, 60, 65, 71, 76, 77, 78 and 79. Nine simplify Sita’s present forest clothing; 52 restores the earlier red sari in an explicitly marked recollection.

## Costume chronology and sources

Departure finery is intentional: local Valmiki passages 2.38.3–6 and 2.39.13–18 allow and supply garments and ornaments. See [sarga 38](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga38/ayodhya_38_prose.htm) and [sarga 39](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/ayodhya/sarga39/ayodhya_39_prose.htm). Plain ochre for everyday forest scenes is a visual continuity choice. Guha’s page-52 earlier-night account matches page 30. Sita’s page-79 wedding memory retains bridal dress; page 78 retains Anasuya’s gift cloth and jewels.
