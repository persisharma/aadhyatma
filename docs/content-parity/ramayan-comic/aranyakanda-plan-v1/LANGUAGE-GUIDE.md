# Respectful language and meaning continuity

This is an authoring guide, with illustrative adapted dialogue to establish tone. It is not the final script or approved translation. Every final line must be checked against its scene/source and speaker/listener relationship.

## Address and grammar rules

- Map speaker, listener, relationship and knowledge state on every dialogue.
- Check pronoun, possessive, verb agreement and tone together, not only honorific names.
- Narration can name Rama and Sita directly; character speech reflects the relationship.
- Respectful disagreement, grief and sourced conflict remain meaningful. No blanket sanitisation or mechanical insertion of ji.
- Use natural authored translations, never transliteration as translation.
- Keep art text-free and derive per-language balloon layout from actual fonts.
- Annotate adapted speech as adaptation, not a verbatim scripture quotation.

## Illustrative dialogue across four languages

### protect-bhabhi — pages 53

Lakshmana → Rama; source intent 3.43.

- **hi:** भैया, मैं सीता भाभी की रक्षा करूँगा।
- **en:** Brother, I will keep Sita Bhabhi safe.
- **gu:** ભાઈ, હું સીતા ભાભીની રક્ષા કરીશ.
- **kn:** ಅಣ್ಣ, ನಾನು ಸೀತಾ ಅತ್ತಿಗೆಯನ್ನು ಕಾಪಾಡುತ್ತೇನೆ.

An acknowledgement adapted from the assigned duty. English introduces Bhabhi as the elder brother’s wife at first use; direct address stays natural thereafter.

### sitas-question — pages 11

Sita → Rama; source intent 3.9.

- **hi:** आर्यपुत्र, बिना कारण किसी से वैर लेना उचित होगा?
- **en:** My lord, would it be right to make an enemy without cause?
- **gu:** આર્યપુત્ર, કારણ વગર કોઈની સાથે વેર લેવું યોગ્ય થશે?
- **kn:** ಆರ್ಯಪುತ್ರ, ಕಾರಣವಿಲ್ಲದೆ ಯಾರೊಂದಿಗಾದರೂ ಹಗೆ ಸಾಧಿಸುವುದು ಸರಿಯೇ?

Respect does not remove Sita’s thoughtful disagreement; Rama’s answer follows on page 13.

### ramas-answer — pages 13

Rama → Sita; source intent 3.10.

- **hi:** सीते, ऋषियों ने रक्षा की प्रार्थना की है। मैं उन्हें दिया वचन निभाऊँगा।
- **en:** Sita, the sages have asked for protection. I will keep the promise I made them.
- **gu:** સીતે, ઋષિઓએ રક્ષા માટે પ્રાર્થના કરી છે. હું તેમને આપેલું વચન પાળીશ.
- **kn:** ಸೀತೇ, ಋಷಿಗಳು ರಕ್ಷಣೆ ಕೇಳಿದ್ದಾರೆ. ನಾನು ಅವರಿಗೆ ಕೊಟ್ಟ ಮಾತನ್ನು ಉಳಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳುತ್ತೇನೆ.

Spousal speech remains warm and natural; no compulsory formal titles in every sentence.

### guard-duty-dilemma — pages 56

Lakshmana → Sita; source intent 3.45.

- **hi:** भाभी, यह छल की पुकार हो सकती है। भैया ने मुझे आपकी रक्षा का दायित्व दिया है।
- **en:** Bhabhi, that cry may be a trick. My brother entrusted your safety to me.
- **gu:** ભાભી, આ પોકાર છળ હોઈ શકે છે. ભાઈએ મને તમારી રક્ષાની જવાબદારી સોંપી છે.
- **kn:** ಅತ್ತಿಗೆ, ಆ ಕೂಗು ಮೋಸವಾಗಿರಬಹುದು. ಅಣ್ಣ ನಿಮ್ಮ ರಕ್ಷಣೆಯ ಹೊಣೆಯನ್ನು ನನಗೆ ನೀಡಿದ್ದಾರೆ.

Retain the two competing duties and the suspected imitation; do not make Lakshmana dismiss her fear.

### panic-and-accusation — pages 57

Sita → Lakshmana; source intent 3.45.

- **hi:** लक्ष्मण, तुम्हारे भैया संकट में हैं। क्या तुम्हें उनकी चिन्ता नहीं?
- **en:** Lakshmana, your brother is in danger. Do you not care about him?
- **gu:** લક્ષ્મણ, તમારા ભાઈ સંકટમાં છે. તમને તેમની ચિંતા નથી?
- **kn:** ಲಕ್ಷ್ಮಣ, ನಿನ್ನ ಅಣ್ಣ ಅಪಾಯದಲ್ಲಿದ್ದಾರೆ. ನಿನಗೆ ಅವರ ಚಿಂತೆ ಇಲ್ಲವೇ?

Tentative child-friendly condensation of a substantially harsher source exchange. Preserve her fear, unfair accusation and his hurt; exact adaptation requires human editorial review. Sita never calls her own husband Bhaiya.

### elder-sage — pages 20

Lakshmana → Agastya-disciple; source intent 3.12.

- **hi:** मुनिवर, राम भैया और सीता भाभी गुरुदेव के दर्शन के लिए आए हैं।
- **en:** Revered sir, my brother Rama and Sita Bhabhi have come to meet the sage.
- **gu:** મુનિવર, રામભાઈ અને સીતા ભાભી ગુરુદેવનાં દર્શન માટે આવ્યાં છે.
- **kn:** ಮುನಿವರ್ಯರೇ, ರಾಮಣ್ಣ ಮತ್ತು ಸೀತಾ ಅತ್ತಿಗೆ ಗುರುಗಳ ದರ್ಶನಕ್ಕೆ ಬಂದಿದ್ದಾರೆ.

Address the gate disciple in his actual role; do not confuse him with Agastya.

### jatayu — pages 81

Rama → Jatayu; source intent 3.68.

- **hi:** तात, सीता को कौन ले गया? वह किस दिशा में गया?
- **en:** Revered elder, who took Sita? Which way did he go?
- **gu:** તાત, સીતાને કોણ લઈ ગયું? તે કઈ દિશામાં ગયું?
- **kn:** ತಂದೆಯವರೇ, ಸೀತೆಯನ್ನು ಯಾರು ಕರೆದೊಯ್ದರು? ಅವರು ಯಾವ ದಿಕ್ಕಿಗೆ ಹೋದರು?

Filial respect for Dasharatha’s friend. Match the final testimony to what Jatayu actually knows, not a full Lanka map.

### calm-the-elder — pages 78

Lakshmana → Rama; source intent 3.65, 3.66.

- **hi:** भैया, पहले दोषी का पता करें। सबको दण्ड देना उचित नहीं।
- **en:** Brother, let us first find who did this. It would be wrong to punish everyone.
- **gu:** ભાઈ, પહેલાં દોષી કોણ છે તે શોધીએ. બધાને દંડ આપવો યોગ્ય નથી.
- **kn:** ಅಣ್ಣ, ಮೊದಲು ತಪ್ಪಿತಸ್ಥ ಯಾರು ಎಂದು ಕಂಡುಹಿಡಿಯೋಣ. ಎಲ್ಲರನ್ನೂ ದಂಡಿಸುವುದು ಸರಿಯಲ್ಲ.

Respectful younger-to-elder disagreement; retain the corrective meaning instead of removing the disagreement.

### shabaris-welfare — pages 89

Rama → Shabari; source intent 3.74.

- **hi:** माता, आप कुशल से हैं? आपकी साधना कैसी चल रही है?
- **en:** Mother, have you been well? How is your spiritual practice progressing?
- **gu:** માતા, આપ કુશળ છો? આપની સાધના કેવી ચાલી રહી છે?
- **kn:** ತಾಯಿಯವರೇ, ನೀವು ಕ್ಷೇಮವಾಗಿದ್ದೀರಾ? ನಿಮ್ಮ ಸಾಧನೆ ಹೇಗೆ ನಡೆಯುತ್ತಿದೆ?

Respect for an elder ascetic; avoid an anonymous fruit-seller introduction.

### marichas-warning — pages 43,48

Maricha → Ravana; source intent 3.31, 3.37.

- **hi:** राजन्, इस योजना को छोड़ दीजिए। इससे बड़ा अनर्थ होगा।
- **en:** Your Majesty, abandon this plan. It will bring disaster.
- **gu:** રાજન્, આ યોજના છોડી દો. તેનાથી મોટો અનર્થ થશે.
- **kn:** ಮಹಾರಾಜರೇ, ಈ ಯೋಜನೆಯನ್ನು ಕೈಬಿಡಿ. ಇದರಿಂದ ದೊಡ್ಡ ಅನರ್ಥವಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.

Courtly address during advice; coercion on page 50 must not be mistaken for willing assent.

### sitas-refusal — pages 60,71

Sita → Ravana; source intent 3.47, 3.56.

- **hi:** मैं तुम्हारा प्रस्ताव स्वीकार नहीं करूँगी।
- **en:** I will not accept your demand.
- **gu:** હું તારો પ્રસ્તાવ સ્વીકારીશ નહીં.
- **kn:** ನಿನ್ನ ಪ್ರಸ್ತಾಪವನ್ನು ನಾನು ಒಪ್ಪುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.

Firm refusal to an aggressor: do not add reverential titles or weaken the lack of consent.
