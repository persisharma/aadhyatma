# Aranyakanda: complete comic plan

2026-10-26. **Planning-only package for review.** No new art, four-language full script, screen implementation or app integration is included.

The plan continues from Ayodhyakanda’s departure from Atri and Anasuya, covers the selected traditional Valmiki Aranyakanda’s entire 75-sarga arc, and ends at Pampa with the purpose of seeking Sugriva. Hanuman’s first meeting and the friendship pact belong to Kishkindha.

## Scope and pacing

**91 provisional pages / 256 scene intents.** This grouping is derived from the causal story beats. The final count can change after source collation and the full four-language scripts; it is not a production quota. This is a whole-arc prose adaptation: repeated battle descriptions, extended genealogies and repeated laments are condensed, while principal actions, motives, responses and consequences are retained. It is not a verse-by-verse transcription. Proposed rows: 3 one-row pages, 16 two-row pages and 72 three-row pages. A hero frame is chosen for a meaningful scene, never to pad a short page. Natural vertical scrolling is allowed. Split or regroup text-heavy narrow panels after measuring all four fonts at Standard and Large sizes.

## Language and narrative locks

Lakshmana addresses Rama as Ram Bhaiya and Sita as Sita Bhabhi, with natural counterparts in Gujarati and Kannada and a once-explained kinship term in English. Pronouns, possessives, verb agreement and tone must match, not just the name. Rama can naturally say Sita/Janaki; Sita’s own husband is never Bhaiya. Elders’ affectionate familiar forms remain natural.

Respect does not remove sourced disagreement or conflict. Sita’s question receives Rama’s answer. Her panic and unfair accusation at the false cry must remain strong enough to explain Lakshmana’s distress and departure. His reply stays respectful. The age-appropriate condensation of that exchange and the Ayomukhi episode need human editorial review. Ravana’s coercion stays explicit; Sita is not blamed for the abduction. No sexualised imagery, graphic mutilation or procedural fire acts are planned.

This follows the selected Valmiki corpus. Lakshmana-rekha, Maya-Sita and tasted-berry details are not silently imported. Sarga 31 is retained with an explicit note about its different treatment in the critical edition. Web commentary and interpretive annexes are not used as scripture dialogue.
