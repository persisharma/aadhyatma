# Bālakāṇḍa card continuity audit

Reviewed the complete 62-page order and all 199 panel placements in `book.json` and `script.json`, reading the English and Hindi copy in sequence and checking page titles, panel order, source references, and transitions across page breaks. All 196 text blocks and 62 titles have the four expected language fields; the Siddhashrama repair is covered by a four-language regression test. This is a narrative continuity pass, not full translation or source collation approval.

## Finding

The confirmed break was on page 17: Rama asked whose hermitage the travellers had reached, then the next card immediately showed Vishnu as Vamana asking Bali for land. The integrated story did not answer Rama or explain why Vishwamitra began that past account. Page 17 now answers that this is Siddhashrama, connects the place to Vishnu's austerities, introduces the Vamana–Bali story as a recollection, and then returns to the travellers arriving at the hermitage. The four language drafts carry the same bridge. Artwork, panel count, and order remain unchanged.
