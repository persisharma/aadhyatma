## Status and next use

The six sheets cover 19 principal identities. They are a first reference set, not the complete cast or every form needed for the entire epic. Ravana's ten-head/twenty-arm full form, child Bharata/Shatrughna, later ruler Lava/Kusha, the queens, Jatayu, Angada, Jambavan, Tara, Kumbhakarna, Indrajit and other episode-specific cast need their own sourced references before those scenes are illustrated.

Use a selected master sheet plus the nearest accepted scene when commissioning a panel. Resolve identity, life stage, dress, form, emotion and props separately. Review the resulting picture; a reference image or repeated prompt does not guarantee consistency.

Production narration/dialogue will be authored in Hindi, English, Gujarati and Kannada. Share text-free scene art across languages; render captions and balloons as text with per-language layout. No translated narrative, production comic page, app route, CDN entry, native resource, upload, commit or PR is included in this milestone.

Before story production, apply [RULEBOOK §29–29.2](../../../RULEBOOK.md) and the [authoring checklist](../kids-story-pacing/authoring-checklist.md): full sourced arc, two publication sources, every transition and all four narratives, each distinct scene's art, hash-bound frame and complete native Standard/Large review. These landscape labelled sheets are not reader assets and must not enter `kidsStoryArtFrames.json` or the CDN manifest. Native checks are not applicable to the reference book and remain pending for the future reader.
