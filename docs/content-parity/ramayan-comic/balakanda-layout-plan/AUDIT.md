## Findings, in reading order

1. **Opening/prologue — needs regrouping.** Current p5 leaves53% empty at Hindi Standard and compresses teaching plus a later royal recital. Proposed pages2–3 separate the emotional cause, Brahma’s instruction, writing, teaching and the clearly labelled later-time court.
2. **Journeys and legends — needs visible causes and timeline labels.** Solitary wide scenes leave repeated gaps. Proposed pages5–45 group related beats; present-day departures/arrivals remain explicit. Reported speech is moved to identified speakers where supported, while legends use short named teller captions.
3. **Conflicts — needs action–response shots.** Current panels such as bk-19-05, bk-20-05/06 and bk-21-04/05 pack gift/attack/defence, rise/fall/rescue or teaching/prayer/reprieve together. New shots separate those existing beats; they add no new plot.
4. **Mithila and marriages — preserve identities, vary emphasis.** Proposed pages46–55 give the bow break and central wedding full hero scenes; the other three couples remain individually named and fully illustrated. No crowd crop or invented vows.
5. **Parashurama and return — dialogue first, complete ending.** Proposed pages57–59 use the approved sample’s conversation anatomy. Pages60–62 restore Varuna’s bow handoff, the embrace, all four brides’ welcome, Yudhajit’s role and the quiet final scene.
6. **Languages and accessibility — plan, not certification.** Locale/size may grow page height; retain page and source-scene anchor. Up to two columns on phone. Screen-reader order must follow row/column and identify speakers. Minimum font/tap, contrast, scaling and gesture behaviour still require native checks.

Strengths retained: source-linked stable identities, reviewed complete foreground frames, distinct character models and the full existing ending. No new reader controls or release changes are made.
