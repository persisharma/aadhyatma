## Layout and dialogue decisions

Eight pages have one row, 19 have two, and 35 have three. Seventy frames carry adapted character dialogue, with silence and concise narration retained where the source does not justify speech. Questions precede legends; motives, responses and consequences remain visible. No invented scripture quotation is claimed. Context captions establish the Narada summary, later Lava/Kusha performance, main-story restart, nested legends and Vishwamitra retrospective. The ending preserves Varuna's bow handoff, Yudhajit's departure and princely life in Ayodhya.

Keep complete painted frames at their measured aspect ratios. Never crop according to caption length or apply a common percentage across assets. Balloon heights follow the actual selected-language font. Very narrow, overly tall balloons widen and flow above complete artwork when required; paired panels do not acquire artificial equal-height paper/sky padding. Page and scene IDs remain stable during language/size changes. Some scroll is accepted to preserve readable text and complete art.
