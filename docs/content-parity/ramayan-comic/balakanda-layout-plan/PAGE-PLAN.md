## Reading contract

- **Dialogue**: 70 proposed speech frames, from existing reported speech/requests/responses. Original planning paraphrases, not scripture quotations. Compare both publication sources before final lettering. No filler chatter, invented romance, curses/prayer texts or motives.
- **Captions**: Name, place, time, an unseen cause or a necessary consequence only. Most action should be visible. Aim at two short caption blocks per page plus name/time labels; exceptions for complex legends/genealogy require an explicit review. Old narration remains a fact checklist, not the new page copy.
- **Translation**: Approve the complete Hindi/English script, then author Gujarati/Kannada against the same stable beats. Review all four full narratives, every transition and the ending. Never use silent English fallback as a translated proof.
- **Art**: Shared text-free art; script-aware live balloons. Plan painted space around identified speakers and complete actions. No empty band, crop-to-fit, stretched faces, tiny text or repeated scenery strips. Match every new/recomposed asset to its own reviewed bounds/hash.
- **Scroll**: Narrative grouping sets page boundaries. Language/size controls may change height while preserving page and stable scene anchor. Targets are estimates; readable copy and complete art override them. If a page exceeds roughly 1.85 reading viewports at Large, first widen the dialogue row or move a complete beat to the next page; never split a reply from its question to hit a cap.
- **Native**: Keep current reader shell, horizontal swipe, typography and safe-area ownership. Browser proof buttons are not a request to add native previous/next. Replace one-panel-per-page data with explicit page.rows and multiple speech entries per frame; keep source IDs and chapter jump anchors. No implementation in this task.
