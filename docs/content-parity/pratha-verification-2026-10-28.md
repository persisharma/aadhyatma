## Publication method

Every retained story below was read in the publications listed on that story’s `referenceUrls`; the row rolls those references up. The Sanskrit Wikisource transcription and a named published translation (Tagare, Shastri, Deshpande or BBT) are separate publications of the passage. They are collated for agreement and differences, not counted as different historical witnesses to a supernatural event. Sites mirroring the same English translation were not counted twice. Folk verification confirms the telling and custom, not historical truth. Reference URLs contain only pages actually opened; unavailable pages and leads belong in draft notes.

No Sanskrit/verse text was entered into the registry. Existing bundled refs remain the only verse-rendering mechanism. Valmiki 6.107 remains excluded.
