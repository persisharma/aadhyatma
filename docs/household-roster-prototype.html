घर की साधना — The Household Practice Roster

PRD-30 (proposed, Q4 round 2) · Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build.
The product already accepted the multi-person household — birth profiles are per-person with a shipped PersonChips roster (#294), Pitru Smaran is per-ancestor. Then data/routine/types.ts defines Routine as { id, nameHi, nameEn, mode, items, createdAt } — no person field. Routines, done-marks, vrat follows and japam alarms all belong to one implicit user, so the second practitioner in the house has no representation at all. This is the only item in the slate that increases people served per install rather than depth per person — and the only one that can break data users already have.
1 · The household day
"Who still has something left today" is a real question in a joint family sharing one phone. Person chips are the shipped component; the roster is the shipped birth-profile list — not a second identity system.

The two real constraints, both visible.
Engineering: iOS holds one shared pending-notification budget, so the planner must allocate across people or a four-person household starves its own reminders. Product: no adherence percentage, ever — that is the failure mode to design out.
Open questions this prototype does not settle
1. The premise itself. This rests on an unvalidated assumption about shared-device usage. If every adult has their own phone, this is wasted work — it is the one item in the slate I would want evidence for before building.
2. The migration. @vedansh/routines and @vedansh/routine-done gain a person dimension while routineItemKey must keep every existing done-mark valid. Write that test first; it is the only thing in round 2 that can break shipped user data.
3. Do vrat follows and japam alarms take personId in the same release as routines, or does v1 scope to routines only? Prototype shows all three; scoping down is the safer v1.
4. Notification allocation policy across people once the shared budget is tight — round-robin, priority by person, or degrade to a single household reminder. Needs a decision before the planner is written.
