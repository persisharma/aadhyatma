कुल परम्परा — Kuldevta, Family Observance & the Tithis of the Living

PRD-29 (proposed, Q4 round 2) · Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build.
The app keeps the tithis of the dead with real care — PRD-17 Pitru Smaran, deriveTithiRuleFromDate, solveNextOccurrence, native-verified reminders — and not one tithi of the living. Multi-person birth profiles shipped in #294 and hold date, time and place for Kundali only; nothing answers "when is my Hindu birthday this year". And kuldev/lineage appear nowhere in source outside katha prose, though the kuldevta is the one piece of practice a family most reliably loses in a generation. Zero new engine work: the janma-tithi half is two existing function calls.
1 · जन्म तिथि — the birthday nothing else in their life tracks
A Hindu birthday is a tithi, not a Gregorian date. The profile already holds the birth date; deriveTithiRuleFromDate yields the rule and solveNextOccurrence yields this year's date — both shipped, tested, and already handling adhik-masa and the paksha edge cases.

Open questions this prototype does not settle
1. gotra is PRD-20's field, and PRD-21 also wants the profile. That is three consumers of one @vedansh:kundali-birth-profile:v1 migration — design it once, in PRD-20. This prototype assumes it already exists.
2. Does the birthday reminder default on or off? Recommended off, opted into per person — it is the shipped notification-preferences pattern and it keeps the shared iOS pending budget honest.
3. Adhik-masa birth tithis and tithi-kshaya recur ambiguously. solveNextOccurrence already resolves this for Pitru Smaran; confirm the same rule reads correctly for the living, where the stakes are lower but the surprise is larger.
4. Kul vrat is linked to a vrat rule so it dates itself. What happens when a family's observance has no rule in festivals.ts? Free text, un-dated, is the assumed fallback.
