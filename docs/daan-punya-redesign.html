What I'd change in code, and what I wouldn't

Everything below is a surface change. The registries, the ledger core, the accessors, the doors and the tests that pin data invariants do not move.

DaanPunyaScreen

Today becomes a hero card carrying the tithi line, the day's causes and the single CTA. The verse spine shows one featured verse (rotating by weekday) with the other principles as fold-open rows. Kathas become a horizontal strip with glyph tiles.

No new routes; no contract change — the home still carries zero give affordances.

DaanJourneyScreen

The stepper becomes a single ScrollView with five marked sections and a sticky progress rail driven by onScroll. The terminal block mounts when the संकल्प section scrolls into view.

Contract change: §27.1 / the journey test currently pins "record and the door do not exist before the last step". It would read "…before the संकल्प section is reached", with a fallback that mounts them on reduced-motion. Your call — this is the one clause that moves.

DaanDirectoryScreen

Unfiltered state becomes a 3×3 grid of cause tiles (glyph, name, place count); the day's causes ring gold. Selecting a tile is the existing filtered state — full mahatva, citation, then places — with a small all-causes chip row on top.

No contract change: chips still derive from rows present; mahatva still precedes places.

Left alone

पात्र-परिचय is already thin after the last commit. खाता and नया दान get the glyph motif on entries and category chips, nothing structural. Doors on More, Observance Detail and PurposeList are unchanged.
