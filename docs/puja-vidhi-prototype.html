पूजा विधि — Guided Step-by-Step Puja Flows

PRD-19 · Interactive UI prototype. Static mockups in the app's parchment system — not the live build.
Entered from the day it's needed — a पूजा विधि action pill on the shipped ObservanceCard (design.md §33.5), beside its existing katha/read pills — not from a new Home category; the catalog lists on the §8 LibraryCard. Two modes: तैयारी (the shipped Today's Practice checklist pattern, day before) and पूजा (one Daily Bhakti-style reading card per step, horizontally paged, keep-awake). Mantras include Devanagari + IAST + read-aloud (#230 TTS); shipped texts (aarti, katha) are handed off by reference, never re-typed. Provenance remains internal review metadata and is not published in the UI.

The festival row in the day panel gains a vidhi action (same mechanism as kathaId); preparation reuses Today's Practice's progress summary, expandable ledger, and circular checks. State persists per festival date.
