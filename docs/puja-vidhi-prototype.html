Entered from the day it's needed — the festival day panel on the Panchang tab (a "पूजा विधि" row beside the existing katha row) — not from a new Home category. Two modes: तैयारी (samagri checklist, day before) and पूजा (full-screen conduct mode, one step per page, keep-awake). Mantras are transcribed from the declared Gita Press convention with IAST + read-aloud (#230 TTS); shipped texts (aarti, katha) are handed off by reference, never re-typed.

Interaction inventory
① Festival day panel → पूजा विधि row (vidhiId hook, beside katha/aarti rows) → vidhi overview. ② तैयारी/पूजा segments — checklist state persists per festival date; day-before vrat reminder resurfaces it. ③ सूची साझा करें → samagri list as plain text (the only share surface). ④ Mantra box: transcribed Devanagari + IAST + read-aloud via shipped TTS; reading-size respected. ⑤ Keep-awake for the whole conduct session, released on exit. ⑥ Paged steps (swipe or bar); × saves state; same-day re-entry offers the readers' resume sheet. ⑦ Shipped-text steps hand off by sectionId (aarti/katha reader + audio) and return. ⑧ Completion: static ॐ seal, katha link, AddToRoutine for recurring vidhis — no celebration animation. ⑨ Catalog reachable from day panel, vrat catalog, search, DISCOVER card — no new Home tile.
