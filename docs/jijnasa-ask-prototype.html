PRD-41 · जिज्ञासा — Ask Vedansh

The resolver in this page is real. Type a question in Devanagari, Hinglish or English and watch it fold, tag entities, score intents, and answer — or abstain.

Its lexicon is generated from the app's own registries — 21 deities from data/deities.ts, 13 occasions from EVENT_RULES, 162 observance rules from panchang/festivals.ts, the 8 dik from DISHA_LABELS — plus a short hand-written alias list. Answer content for bhog, vrat food and vastu is the verbatim shipped text from bhogContent.ts and data/vastu/roomGuidance.ts. Dates and times in the panchang/muhurat cards are illustrative: the shipped card renders whatever the engine returns.

अवधिमार्च 2027 तक
Every card here is a standing question. Which is why Phase 2 costs weeks and not a quarter — the engine is Phase 1's, and the only new work is composition and the launch-path discipline in §13.7.
What this prototype is honest about. The resolver, the lexicon and the folding are real and run in this page. The bhog, vrat-food and vastu answers are the shipped verified text, provenance included. Panchang and muhurat values are illustrative — wiring them would mean running the astronomy engine in a browser, which proves nothing the engine tests don't already prove. The trace panel is a review tool, not a shipped surface.

Known gaps, deliberately left visible. Three, all Phase 0 work, and all findable in the trace panel:
· Class vs. instance — ask एकादशी कब है and it answers with a specific named Ekadashi rather than the next one of any name (PRD §13.3). Recurring families need to be modelled as classes.
· Over-eager stemming — ask राहु काल and the trace tags deity=kali, because काल stems into kali. Harmless here (the intent takes no deity slot) but it is the same mechanism that mis-tags vrat me kya khaye to a specific vrat rule. The fix is a specificity floor: generic tokens (vrat · kaal · puja · din) must never entity-tag on their own.
· Unseen phrasings abstain rather than guess (§13.5) — which is correct behaviour, and still a recall gap.

Every one of these was found by running the thing rather than describing it. That is the argument for Phase 0 shipping a golden corpus before any UI.
