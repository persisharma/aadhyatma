Panchang values, muhurat windows and sunrise and sunset times are computed by an astronomy engine and are provided for reference. They may differ from your local almanac or family tradition, which use different reckonings and rounding. Use them as a guide, not as a ruling — for consequential decisions, consult your family priest or astrologer.
