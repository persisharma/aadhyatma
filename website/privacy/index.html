Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Vedansh is built to respect your privacy completely. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no tracking. The app works fully offline, and what you read, count and keep stays on your device.

In one line: we collect nothing about you. There is no server holding your data, because there is no server.

Information we collect

None. Vedansh has no sign-up or login, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking SDKs. No information about you or how you use the app is collected, stored or transmitted to us.

Location

Location access is optional and, when granted, is used only while you are using the app, to compute accurate sunrise, sunset and Panchang timings for where you are. Your coordinates are processed on your device only and are never transmitted or stored off it. If you decline location, a manual city picker (Ujjain by default) gives you the same functionality.

Notifications and alarms

Daily reminders and japam alarms are scheduled locally on your device. Vedansh does not use push-notification servers, so no device token is created and no notification data reaches us or any third party. On Android, the app asks for exact-alarm and start-on-boot permissions purely so a japam alarm you set still fires at the right minute after a restart.

Audio and microphone

The app plays mantras, aartis and bhajans, including in the background, from audio bundled inside the app. It does not use the microphone and does not record anything.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, routines, Sankalp programmes, japam counts and streaks are saved in local storage on your device so the app remembers your place. This data never leaves your device, is never backed up to us, and is removed when you delete the app.

App updates

So that improvements can reach you without waiting for a store release, the app checks for updates from Expo's update service (u.expo.dev). That check carries only what any network request carries — your IP address, the app version and the platform — and no personal data, no identifier we control, and nothing about your practice. It is the only outbound connection the app makes.

Children's privacy

Because Vedansh collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

We do not share, sell or disclose personal data — because we do not collect any. No third party receives information about you through this app. The App Store and Google Play collect their own download and crash statistics under their own policies, which we cannot see at an individual level.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above is revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to vedansh.app.support@gmail.com.
