## Summary

Home has a Stories for Kids card with a deity-neutral picture-book icon. It opens the deity catalog, then a deity's story shelf, then the illustrated paged reader. Krishna has Krishna Janma, Putana and Kaliya Nag; Ganesha has Ganesha Birth; Hanuman has Hanuman and the Sun. The previously disabled Kaliya teaser is now a reader story.

## Details

- `KidsStoriesHomeCard` sits between Categories and Discover. Its small bundled icon hints at all three deity shelves; it does not load story JSON on Home's static launch path.
- `KidsStoryLibraryScreen` lists `storyDeities` with the shared `DeityCard` (glyph, active gradient and bilingual title hierarchy) and derives published story counts from `storiesForDeity`. Editorially authored Gujarati/Kannada names override generic transliteration. All three shelves now have reader stories.
- `KidsStoryDeityScreen` filters `kidsStories` by `deityId`. Published cover cards use the active library palette and bilingual title hierarchy and open `KidsStoryReader`; `plannedStories` entries display without a press action or reader route. Back follows the same Home stack: reader → shelf → deity catalog → Home.
- `krishna-janma.json` contains the four-language story, stable page ids, artwork keys and source notes. `KidsStoryArt` maps those keys to seventeen bundled WebP files: one cover and sixteen distinct scenes. Wedding, threat, imprisonment, Balarama, prayer and return use `kj-12`–`kj-17`. App and browser asset copies must match byte-for-byte; the data test guards distinct scenes and prototype parity. Promise-scene palms and Devi's eight connected arms/held objects have been corrected. The closing “Safe in Gokul” page uses its own `kj-11.webp`, showing Yashoda and baby Krishna, while the cover remains the prison birth scene.
- Browser prototypes live in `docs/kids-stories-home-prototype.html`, `docs/kids-stories-catalog-prototype.html` and `docs/kids-stories-prototype.html`. The reusable illustration recipe is `docs/content-parity/kids-stories-art-style.md`.
- The four added JSON records have 6/7/7/6 scenes and authored hi/en/gu/kn text. They share the existing reader without changing its paging or caption layout. Each scene has a distinct WebP: `pt-01`–`pt-06`, `ka-01`–`ka-07`, `gb-01`–`gb-07`, `hs-01`–`hs-06`. Covers reuse representative interior scenes, never the final scene. The four matching `docs/kids-stories-{id}-prototype.html` copies are regenerated by `mobile/scripts/sync-kids-story-prototypes.mjs`; tests pin content and byte parity.
- New records carry dated `source` publication provenance and page references. `docs/content-parity/kids-stories-expansion/sources.md` documents adaptation boundaries; `artwork.json` records generation and visual review. Ganesha follows Shiva Purana's Shvetakalpa account (human head before restoration, single-tusked elephant head afterwards). Hanuman follows Uttara-kanda 35–36 (flight towards the sun, four-tusked Airavata), without importing the sun-swallowing folk version. No graphic injury or invented liturgical text is illustrated or added.
- `.maestro/kids-stories-new-stories-smoke.yaml` visits all 26 added scenes, checks counters and language stability at each ending, and returns through the shelves. The native Release build and all three story smoke flows passed on iOS 26.5. The regional smoke flow checks every added scene in Gujarati and Kannada at Large reading size. Native crop review caught a long Kannada Kaliya caption hiding Krishna’s face; the narration was shortened without removing the coils, Balarama’s restraint or Krishna’s escape. Source/assets tests, reader tests and native smoke evidence have separate scopes; regional editorial review remains a release check. Completion evidence lives in `docs/content-parity/kids-stories-expansion/validation.md`.

## Gotchas

- Planned titles are catalog teasers, not published stories. Do not add them to `kidsStories` until sourced text, all translations, artwork and review are ready.
- Keep story text as native text below text-free art. Share artwork across languages, preserve the page index when changing language, and inspect the final image against its actual page text.
- The library and reader routes are lazy on the Home stack. Re-run `mobile/scripts/gen-route-graph.mts` after changing navigation edges and keep the launch graph test green.
