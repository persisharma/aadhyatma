## Summary

Home has a Stories for Kids card with a deity-neutral picture-book icon. It opens the deity catalog, then a deity's story shelf, then the illustrated paged reader. Krishna has Krishna Janma, Putana and Kaliya Nag; Ganesha has Ganesha Birth; Hanuman has Hanuman and the Sun. The previously disabled Kaliya teaser is now a reader story.

## Details

- `KidsStoriesHomeCard` sits between Categories and Discover. Its small bundled icon hints at all three deity shelves; it does not load story JSON on Home's static launch path.
- `KidsStoryLibraryScreen` lists `storyDeities` with the shared `DeityCard` (glyph, active gradient and bilingual title hierarchy) and derives published story counts from `storiesForDeity`. Editorially authored Gujarati/Kannada names override generic transliteration. All three shelves now have reader stories.
- `KidsStoryDeityScreen` filters `kidsStories` by `deityId`. Published cover cards use the active library palette and bilingual title hierarchy and open `KidsStoryReader`; `plannedStories` entries display without a press action or reader route. Back follows the same Home stack: reader → shelf → deity catalog → Home.
- `krishna-janma.json` contains the four-language story, stable page ids, artwork keys and source notes. `KidsStoryArt` maps those keys to seventeen WebP illustrations (one cover + sixteen distinct scenes) **served from the R2 CDN** — each is downloaded once and cached on-device (`assetCache` / `useCachedAsset`, keyed through `kidsStoryAssetManifest.json`), offline after first view, with the parchment slot shown during a first fetch. (`story-library.webp`, the Home-card art, stays bundled.) Wedding, threat, imprisonment, Balarama, prayer and return use `kj-12`–`kj-17`. App and browser asset copies must match byte-for-byte; the data test guards distinct scenes and prototype parity. Promise-scene palms and Devi's eight connected arms/held objects have been corrected. The closing “Safe in Gokul” page uses its own `kj-11.webp`, showing Yashoda and baby Krishna, while the cover remains the prison birth scene.
- Browser prototypes live in `docs/kids-stories-home-prototype.html`, `docs/kids-stories-catalog-prototype.html` and `docs/kids-stories-prototype.html`. The reusable illustration recipe is `docs/content-parity/kids-stories-art-style.md`.
- The four added JSON records have 10/8/11/10 scenes and authored hi/en/gu/kn text. Each starts with a sourced, distinct illustrated character/setting page before the existing action. Putana's next page introduces Kansa's fear; original page IDs, covers and scenes remain stable. They share the existing reader without changing its paging or caption layout. Each scene has a distinct WebP: `pt-00`–`pt-09`, `ka-00`–`ka-07`, `gb-00`–`gb-10`, `hs-00`–`hs-09`. Covers reuse representative interior scenes, never the final scene. The four matching `docs/kids-stories-{id}-prototype.html` copies are regenerated by `mobile/scripts/sync-kids-story-prototypes.mjs`; tests pin content and byte parity.
- New records carry dated `source` publication provenance and page references. `docs/content-parity/kids-stories-expansion/sources.md` documents adaptation boundaries; `artwork.json` records generation and visual review. Ganesha follows Shiva Purana's Shvetakalpa account (human head before restoration, single-tusked elephant head afterwards). Hanuman follows Uttara-kanda 35–36 (flight towards the sun, four-tusked Airavata), without importing the sun-swallowing folk version. No graphic injury or invented liturgical text is illustrated or added.
- The complete-arc revision separates Putana’s intention/cry/collapse/rescue, Ganesha’s decision/conflict/plea, and Hanuman’s hunger/Rahu’s appeal/the world’s appeal to Brahma. Nine distinct new illustrations bring the four added stories to 39 scenes. Source, narrative transition tables, illustration prompts and measured payload (2,240,225 bytes) are in `docs/content-parity/kids-story-pacing/`.
- `KidsStoryReaderScreen` nests a vertical ScrollView per scene inside the horizontal pager. `KidsStoryArt` preserves a width-derived 4:5 illustration independent of caption length, with `contain` and no flex shrinking. All 56 bundled illustrations have per-art reviewed frames in `kidsStoryArtFrames.json`, retaining the top 78–88% and trimming only blank parchment. The source-byte SHA-256 gate requires a new frame review when art changes; unreviewed future art stays full-height. `hs09` keeps the people, cow, village and deities at 82% retained height. The visible tab bar owns the bottom safe-area inset; the reader excludes that edge to avoid a duplicate blank strip. An 8-point scene gap/bottom inset and 12-point caption vertical padding avoid unnecessary overflow. Longer captions scroll rather than cropping faces or supporting characters. This replaces the old fit-to-screen art slot.

The complete final Standard/Large automated framing run remains incomplete. ## Gotchas

- Planned titles are catalog teasers, not published stories. Do not add them to `kidsStories` until sourced text, all translations, artwork and review are ready.
- AGENTS.md directs all new/revised story work to RULEBOOK §29, which requires a complete sourced arc and a checked-in page-by-page transition review before art sign-off. Use the reusable authoring checklist; read every complete locale and inspect the native sequence, not just the opening. Tests cannot establish narrative comprehension.
- Keep story text as native text below text-free art. Share artwork across languages, preserve the page index when changing language, and inspect the final image against its actual page text.
- The library and reader routes are lazy on the Home stack. Re-run `mobile/scripts/gen-route-graph.mts` after changing navigation edges and keep the launch graph test green.
