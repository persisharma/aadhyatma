## Summary

Krishna has Krishna Janma, Putana and Kaliya Nag; Ganesha has Ganesha Birth; Hanuman has Hanuman and the Sun. ## Gotchas

- Planned titles are catalog teasers, not published stories. Do not add them to `kidsStories` until sourced text, all translations, artwork and review are ready.
- AGENTS.md directs all new/revised story work to RULEBOOK §29, which requires a complete sourced arc and a checked-in page-by-page transition review before art sign-off. Use the reusable authoring checklist; read every complete locale and inspect the native sequence, not just the opening. Tests cannot establish narrative comprehension.
- Covers use a width-only 120pt wrapper: height derives from their reviewed art frame, with no fixed 150pt slot. Catalogs use one 12pt bottom padding above the tab bar. The Durga follow-up records all 87 individual pixel boundaries and 14 reused covers in `docs/content-parity/kids-stories-durga/layout-review.json`; original native evidence is explicitly historical.
- Every published story now has multi-card sharing via the app-wide ShareProvider, built by `kidsStoryShareable` (exported from `shareContent.ts`, implemented in `kidsStoryShare.ts`). Each scene is one card: complete scene art above the title and full fixed-size caption/dialogue (the paginator reserves a 160 dp minimum art box and the art grows into what the caption leaves); takeaway and source note close on a plain card. Authored four-language captions/dialogue are retained. Aligned numbered parts stay within ten cards across all locales; a current-scene scope follows the visible reader page. R2/cache resolution and native image decode must both succeed before an illustrated capture.

- RULEBOOK §29.1 applies the image/layout gate to every new illustrated story section. The current generation template no longer requests a blank lower fifth or caption area; historical prompts are provenance. Plan a supported frame against the actual viewport, review each image's actual bounds/hash (full height when no trim is needed), and diagnose source whitespace, aspect-ratio letterboxing, fixed wrappers, padding and duplicate insets separately. Legacy crop percentages are not new-art presets. Current `KidsStoryArt` assumes 4:5, so another ratio requires renderer/caller verification. The copyable implementation request is `docs/content-parity/story-image-layout-prompt.md`.
- Keep story text as native text below text-free art. Share artwork across languages, preserve the page index when changing language, and inspect the final image against its actual page text.
- The library and reader routes are lazy on the Home stack. Re-run `mobile/scripts/gen-route-graph.mts` after changing navigation edges and keep the launch graph test green.

The browser readers and catalog derive per-image aspect frames from `kidsStoryArtFrames.json` through `sync-kids-story-prototypes.mjs`; complete artwork sits at its natural source ratio behind the reviewed bottom boundary. Native share cards use measured title height rather than a fixed title reserve. The source/reader/cover/export evidence and remaining scroll are kept in the Durga validation directory.
